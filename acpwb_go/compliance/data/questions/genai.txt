# Generative AI at work.
Q: Before pasting text into a generative AI tool, you should check:
+ Whether the tool is approved for that type of data
- Whether the tool has a friendly name
- Whether your coworker uses it
- Nothing

Q: Generative AI can produce confident answers that are false. This is often called:
+ Hallucination
- Optimization
- Encryption
- Compliance

Q: Output from a generative AI tool used in customer communications must be:
+ Reviewed by a person for accuracy before it is sent
- Sent as-is to save time
- Signed by the AI tool
- Translated into binary

Q: Pasting a customer contract into a free, public AI chatbot is:
+ Not allowed; use only approved tools for confidential data
- Fine if you delete the chat later
- Encouraged for faster reviews
- Required by Legal

Q: Using AI to write code for production requires:
+ The same review, testing, and license checks as any other code
- No review because AI is perfect
- Removing all comments
- Approval from the AI

Q: AI-generated images used in marketing should:
+ Follow brand, copyright, and disclosure guidance
- Include real people's faces without consent
- Copy a competitor's ad style exactly
- Be used without review

Q: Using AI to make decisions about hiring or promotion:
+ Needs review for bias and compliance with employment laws
- Removes all legal risk
- Is required by policy
- Is fine without human oversight

Q: If an AI tool gives you a citation for a legal or regulatory claim, you should:
+ Verify that the source exists and says what the tool claims
- Assume it is correct
- Add more citations to be safe
- Remove the date

Q: Prompts and outputs in an approved enterprise AI tool are:
+ Company records subject to the same policies as other business data
- Private and exempt from all policies
- Deleted by law every hour
- Owned by the AI

Q: Creating fake reviews or testimonials with AI is:
+ Deceptive and prohibited
- Creative marketing
- Allowed if the product is good
- Required for launches

Q: If you are unsure whether an AI use is allowed, you should:
+ Check the AI acceptable use policy or ask the AI governance team
- Ask the AI if it is allowed
- Proceed and see what happens
- Use a personal account instead
