# Offboarding and departures.

Q: When an employee leaves ACPWB, their system access should be removed:
+ Promptly, on or before their last day
- Within six months
- Only if they ask
- After the farewell lunch

Q: A departing employee may take with them:
+ Their personal items and personal copies of their own pay and benefits documents
- Customer lists for their next job
- Their laptop as a gift
- Confidential project files for their portfolio

Q: Emailing confidential files to your personal email before you resign is:
+ A policy violation that may also be illegal
- A smart backup
- Allowed for your own work
- Allowed if you delete them later

Q: An exit interview is meant to:
+ Gather honest feedback about the employee's experience
- Convince the employee to stay at all costs
- Assign blame
- Collect the employee's passwords

Q: Final pay must be provided:
+ By the deadline set by state law, which in some states is the last day of work
- Whenever convenient
- After the employee returns their badge, even if that is months later
- Only if the employee asks twice

Q: A manager announces a departure to the team. The announcement should:
+ Be respectful and avoid sharing the reason for leaving unless the employee agreed
- Explain every reason in detail
- Include a critique of the employee
- Be posted with a party popper emoji if the departure was involuntary

Q: Accounts owned by a departing employee, such as shared files and recurring meetings, should be:
+ Transferred to another owner before access is removed
- Deleted immediately
- Left for the employee to manage from home
- Given to {VENDOR}

Q: A former employee calls asking for a coworker's personal phone number. You should:
+ Not share it; personal contact information is confidential
- Share it, since they used to work here
- Share it if they sound friendly
- Text it to them

Q: Reference requests for former employees should be handled by:
+ HR, following the reference policy
- Any coworker who remembers them
- Their least favorite manager
- The {COMMITTEE}

Q: Badges returned by departing employees should be:
+ Deactivated and collected by Security
- Given to the next new hire
- Kept by the manager for spare use
- Mailed to {CITY}
