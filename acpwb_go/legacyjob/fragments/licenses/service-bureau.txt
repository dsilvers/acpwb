SERVICE BUREAU PROCESSING AGREEMENT

Client shall deliver input decks to the {HOLDER} data control window, {PLACE}, by 4 PM on the processing day. Decks delivered after 4 PM will be processed the following business day, or will be processed the same day and reported as the following business day.

{HOLDER} will process the deck using {PROGRAM} and deliver printed output by courier the next morning. Client acknowledges that the Program and its methods are confidential and that Client receives only the Output.

{HOLDER} is not responsible for decks that are dropped, bent, folded, spindled or mutilated in transit. Client should number its cards.
