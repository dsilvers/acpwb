# Whiteboards, shredding, keys, and lockers.

Q: Before leaving a meeting room, you should:
+ Erase the whiteboard and collect any papers you brought
- Leave the whiteboard for the next meeting
- Take a photo of the whiteboard and post it
- Write "DO NOT ERASE" on everything

Q: A whiteboard in a glass-walled room facing the hallway should:
+ Not display confidential information visible to passers-by
- Display the merger plan for visibility
- Display passwords for convenience
- Be decorated

Q: Confidential paper documents should be disposed of:
+ In locked shredding bins
- In the regular trash
- In open recycling bins
- In the {PLACE} sink

Q: Torn-up confidential documents placed in a trash can are:
+ Not securely destroyed
- Fully destroyed
- Encrypted
- Approved by {AGENCY}

Q: Physical keys to ACPWB offices and cabinets should be:
+ Signed out, kept secure, and returned when no longer needed
- Copied for friends
- Hidden under the doormat
- Labeled with the room they open and left on desks

Q: A lost office key should be reported:
+ Promptly, so locks can be evaluated and changed if needed
- Never
- At year end
- Only if it was a master key

Q: Day lockers should be:
+ Emptied at the end of the day
- Claimed permanently
- Used to store food for weeks
- Used to store confidential files unlocked

Q: A shredding bin that is full should be:
+ Reported to Facilities for pickup, not overfilled or emptied into recycling
- Emptied into the recycling bin
- Stuffed with more paper
- Left with papers stacked on top

Q: The combination to a secure cabinet should be:
+ Shared only with authorized people and changed when someone leaves
- Written on the cabinet
- Posted on {SYSTEM}
- The same as the office door code

Q: Printing documents and leaving them on the printer is:
+ A confidentiality risk; collect printouts immediately or use secure print release
- Fine during business hours
- Required for shared printing
- A clean desk best practice
