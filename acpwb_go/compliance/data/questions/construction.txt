# Renovations and construction zones in occupied offices.

Q: Barricaded construction areas in the office should be:
+ Entered only by authorized people with the required PPE
- Used as shortcuts
- Used for storage
- Explored at lunch

Q: Dust or strong odors from renovation work should be:
+ Reported to Facilities
- Ignored
- Covered with air freshener
- Reported to {VENDOR}

Q: Contractors working in the office should:
+ Wear visible badges and be escorted or authorized for the areas they work in
- Have unrestricted access everywhere
- Borrow employee badges
- Use the executive restrooms only

Q: A temporary exit route during construction should be:
+ Clearly marked and kept unobstructed
- Blocked for the duration
- Known only to Facilities
- Changed daily without notice

Q: Fire alarms or sprinkler systems that are shut off during construction require:
+ A fire watch or other approved precautions until they are restored
- Nothing
- Ignoring the alarm if it goes off
- Covering the detectors permanently

Q: Older buildings undergoing renovation may contain:
+ Asbestos or lead paint that must be managed by qualified contractors
- Treasure
- Nothing hazardous
- Hidden {EVENT} decorations

Q: Construction noise near your desk:
+ May allow you to work elsewhere temporarily; ask your manager or Facilities
- Must be endured silently
- Is grounds for a raise
- Can be stopped by asking the workers

Q: Walking under a ladder or scaffolding where work is being done is:
+ Not allowed; walk around the work area
- Allowed if you hurry
- Allowed if you wear a hat
- Good luck

Q: A "hot work" permit is required for:
+ Welding, cutting, or other spark-producing work, with fire precautions
- Working in summer
- Coffee brewing
- Using laptops outside

Q: Construction debris left in hallways should be:
+ Reported so it can be cleared from egress routes
- Stepped over
- Used as seating
- Painted

Q: Contractors may use ACPWB tools and equipment:
+ Only as agreed in their contract
- Freely
- Only on weekends
- After asking any employee

Q: Fire doors propped open by contractors should be:
+ Closed and reported to Facilities
- Left open for airflow
- Removed
- Decorated
