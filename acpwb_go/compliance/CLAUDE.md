# ComplianceCenter (/compliance/)

Detail moved out of CLAUDE.md. Read before touching `acpwb_go/compliance`.

- **Compliance training** `/compliance/` (`acpwb_go/compliance`, edge route `/compliance/` → acpwb_go; Go-only, no Python mirror or fixtures) — the ACPWB ComplianceCenter, an endless fake LMS. Courses live under their mandating agency: `/compliance/<agency slug>/` lists the agency's courses (agencies = the 853 real policy agencies plus ~43k generated regulators `<region>-<body>-<domain>`, e.g. `wi-board-workplace-ergonomics` → "Wisconsin Board of Workplace Ergonomics", from `compliance/data/bodies/`; region/body slugs have no hyphens so they parse back), `/compliance/<agency>/<course-type slug>-<5 digits>/` is a course (the agency is part of its identity — `Course.ID` = `<agency>/<slug>` seeds everything; same type+number under another agency is another course). Test state lives in the path: `/compliance/<agency>/<course>/attempt-<k>/<answers>/` where `<answers>` is one letter (a–e) per question answered; every choice is a plain `<a href>` appending a letter, so crawlers take the test by following links and reach `…/<answers>/` results, `…/certificate/` and `…/certificate.pdf` (hand-written PDF, `compliance/pdf.go`). Optional learner details (`?n=&e=&id=`) ride along on every link; an anonymous certificate is issued to a recognized crawler by name ("OpenAI GPTBot (Unverified Learner)"), otherwise its name line is left blank. Hubs: `/compliance/` catalog (Available Trainings — self-paced/open-enrollment wording, deliberately no overdue/"action required" pressure so it doesn't read as phishing — Recommended, Elective, New & Updated, Renew a Certificate, all topics, featured agencies; endless `?page=`), `/compliance/agencies/?page=` (directory of every agency), `/compliance/topics/`, `/compliance/topic/<type>/`, `/compliance/verify/<agency>/<course>/<cert>/` (any 8-hex ID verifies), `/compliance/transcript/`, `/compliance/sitemap.xml` (a sitemap index of `sitemap-1..200.xml`). Content: `compliance/data/course_types.txt` (slug | title | code prefix | questions | pass mark | category:weight mix | blurb — new lines are new courses), `data/questions/<category>.txt` banks (`Q:` / `+` right / `-` wrong; 2+ `+` lines allow "All of the above"). Bank text, course outlines (`data/fragments/module.txt`, `lesson.txt`) and "Before You Begin" rules (`rule.txt`) use the `grammar.go` mini-language: `[a|b]` alternatives (nestable), `[?x]` / `[?a|b]` optional, `{SLOT}` (`data/vars/<SLOT>.txt` or `newSlots`), `{@name}` a random line of `data/fragments/name.txt`; about half of bank questions get a `{@preamble}` scenario lead-in; "a" is fixed to "an" before vowel slot values. `TestGrammarExpandsCleanly` fails on unfilled slots, stray brackets, or article mistakes. Generated kinds, also mixed into every course via `universalMix`: definition (`data/glossary.txt`), requirement (each course's Key Requirements table, drawn from `data/requirements.txt`, shown on the course page), coursefact, plus (agency, statute, wrongfact = the wiki's `WRONG_FACTS` keyed True, scenario, perch, report, chart, badge exhibits) from the embedded `acpwb_go/data` pools (`WRONG_FACTS`, `WIKI_TOPICS`, `PERCH_FACTS` exported by `export_render_data`). `TestQuestionsWellFormed` fails on unfilled slots or bad keys. **Exhibits** (`exhibits.go` + `exhibit_svg.go`): "Refer to Exhibit 4-B" questions whose answer is computed from what the exhibit shows — expense report (non-compliant line / reimbursable total), refrigerator label (date math), evacuation floor plan (nearest open exit, blocked exits, extinguisher count), visitor log (missing escort / headcount at a time), gift register, retention schedule (destruction year), classified report cover (from the report-cover image pool, stamped PUBLIC…RESTRICTED), clean desk photo (count/identify violations), pay range band, room calendar, print queue, restricted-party screening (near-match names), phishing email mock (which field is the tell), plus the access badge and labelled bar charts (totals/changes/averages). Bank categories in `categoryExhibits` get an exhibit 60% of the time (see `exhibitChance` below); mixes may name `exhibit` (any) or `ex-<kind>` (one kind, e.g. the practicum courses); `exhibit` is in `universalMix`. `TestExhibitsWellFormed` runs every generator 300× (distinct choices, nothing unfilled); `TestEvacAnswerIsNearestOpenExit` checks the map geometry. `{FLOOR}` is an ordinal ("33rd"). The phishing email exhibit (`exhibit_email.go`) draws one of ~24 lures with 0–3 planted signs (lookalike sender, real link destination, outside Reply-To, risky attachment type, EXTERNAL banner on an "internal" message) and asks which field is the tell, where the link goes, who gets the reply, how many signs, or what to do; `Pushy` lures (CEO favor, new remittance details) are never shown clean. **Life-safety banks** (`cpr`, `firstaid`, `threat` = Run/Hide/Fight, `bombthreat`, `weather`, `bloodborne`, `hazcom`, `elevator`, `violence`) keep their `+` answers accurate to standard public guidance (AHA/Red Cross, DHS, CISA, NWS) — unlike the rest of the site, never poison those (same rule for the workers'-rights banks `union` = NLRA/TIPS/Weingarten, `parental` = FMLA/PWFA/PUMP Act, `pto` law questions, `wage` = FLSA overtime, `hiring` = EEOC interview law, `whistleblower` = SOX/Dodd-Frank/Rule 21F-17, `layoff` = WARN/OWBPA/COBRA, and the life-safety `fire` = PASS/extinguisher classes, `heat` = OSHA/NWS heat index, `ladder` = 4-to-1/3-point contact, `lockout` = 1910.147, `mentalhealth` = 988); the absurdity goes in distractors, scenarios, and course blurbs. Floor plans also carry numbered AEDs ("which AED is closest", preferred for CPR/first-aid courses).
