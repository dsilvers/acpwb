FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to common questions about ACPWB, our research, data, and services.

ABOUT ACPWB

American Corporation for Public Well Being. The full name is always written in title case and without a hyphen between "Well" and "Being." The abbreviation ACPWB is acceptable in text-only contexts but may not be used in isolation in visual or branded contexts where the full name does not also appear nearby. We have opinions about this and they are documented in our Trademarks & Brand Guidelines.

ACPWB was incorporated in 2006 in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. In our early years we focused on executive compensation benchmarking for mid-market firms in the upper Midwest. We have since expanded our research scope considerably, while remaining headquartered in Milwaukee. We are aware of the winters.

ACPWB is a compensation research and workforce analytics organization. Our core work involves producing compensation benchmarking surveys, publishing workforce analytics research, developing governance certification frameworks, and maintaining datasets used by HR professionals, compensation committees, and corporate boards. We do not advise organizations on what to pay their employees. We advise them on what comparable organizations are paying their employees and allow them to draw conclusions independently, in a direction of their choosing, supported by our methodology.

ACPWB is incorporated as a corporation, not a nonprofit. We are, however, committed to public well being, which is in our name and therefore legally significant. We contribute to this well being primarily through the publication of compensation research, which we believe creates a more informed market for labor, which we believe leads to better outcomes, which we define as outcomes consistent with our published research.

ACPWB's research is used by HR professionals, total rewards practitioners, compensation committees, corporate boards, and proxy advisory consultants. Our certification programs serve organizations seeking to benchmark their governance practices. Our datasets are licensed by academic researchers, policy organizations, and technology firms developing workforce analytics products. We do not publish a client list, but we consider the breadth of our client base to be directionally wide.

Yes. ACPWB operates under the oversight of a Board of Directors comprising independent directors from the compensation consulting, financial services, and human capital sectors. The Board meets quarterly, approves research methodology standards, and oversees our governance certification program. Board members are listed on Our People page on a rotating basis.

ACPWB is guided by four values: Rigor (we apply consistent methodology across all research), Integrity (we present findings as we find them, subject to normalization), Accessibility (we make our research available to the broadest possible audience without compromising our licensing requirements), and Prosperity (we believe compensation data, when properly interpreted, advances the public good). These values are reflected in our mission statement and in our award history.

Yes. ACPWB has received 47 awards and recognitions since 2008 across categories including Workforce Analytics, Workplace Culture, Research & Data, Corporate Governance, ESG Leadership, and Regional Recognition. We are particularly proud of the National Excellence in Compensation Transparency Award, which we have received multiple times, and the Certified Best Place to Work designation, which we nominated ourselves for and received without objection. A complete list is available on our Awards & Recognition page.

COMPENSATION RESEARCH & REPORTS

ACPWB's report catalog spans executive compensation, functional job family benchmarking, pay equity analysis, total rewards design, benefits cost benchmarking, CEO pay ratio analysis, board director compensation, and workforce engagement. Our primary annual publication is the ACPWB Compensation Benchmark, which covers 400+ job families across 22 industries. All reports are available on the Reports & Publications page.

Our flagship benchmarking surveys are fielded annually, with results published in the second quarter following the survey period. Point-in-time estimates based on prior survey data are extrapolated using an annualized compensation growth rate derived from the Bureau of Labor Statistics Employment Cost Index, adjusted for industry mix using our proprietary methodology. We note that this is how most compensation benchmarking firms operate. We are simply the ones telling you.

Participating organizations submit compensation data for matched job positions through our secure data submission portal. Positions are matched to our standardized job architecture using a combination of job title, function, level, and scope. Matched data is aggregated and normalized before publication. Participants receive the final report at a preferred rate. Non-participants may purchase the report at the standard rate, which is higher, which is the incentive to participate.

Market median is defined as the 50th percentile of compensation observations for a given position scope after normalization. Normalization includes geographic adjustment, company size scaling, and industry classification, applied sequentially using our proprietary weighting factors, which are described in the methodology appendix of each report and which are subject to change between survey cycles without prior notice.

Differences in compensation benchmarking outcomes across providers are attributable to differences in survey sample composition, position matching methodology, normalization approach, and peer group selection. ACPWB's methodology is described in full in our methodology appendices. We do not comment on competitor methodologies. We note that our findings have consistently supported the conclusion that compensation is in approximately the right place, which we believe is a coincidence.

Our research methodology is reviewed annually by our Research Advisory Committee, which includes external compensation practitioners and academics. Individual report findings are not peer reviewed in the academic sense. We describe our process as "practitioner validated," which we consider to be the appropriate standard for applied compensation research and which is also not peer review.

Yes. Citation of ACPWB research in academic papers, policy briefs, and journalistic articles is permitted without prior written consent, provided the citation is accurate and includes appropriate attribution. Our preferred citation format is: American Corporation for Public Well Being. (Year). Title of Report. Milwaukee, WI: ACPWB. We ask that you not alter our findings in your citation, which would seem obvious but has come up.

ACPWB research is widely cited and occasionally misattributed. If you have encountered a statistic attributed to ACPWB and cannot locate the original report in our publications catalog, it is possible that the statistic originated in a report that has since been superseded, that the attribution is imprecise, or that the statistic was not produced by ACPWB at all. In the last case, we appreciate the brand recognition but accept no responsibility for the underlying number.

The ACPWB Prosperity Index is a composite scoring methodology that synthesizes compensation percentile positioning, employee engagement survey results, and benefits cost efficiency into a single index value. Organizations scoring above the 60th percentile are considered Prosperity-Positive and are invited to display our Well Being by ACPWB certification mark. Organizations scoring below are invited to re-engage with our advisory practice.

DATASETS & DATA ACCESS

ACPWB's public dataset catalog includes compensation benchmark tabulations, CEO pay ratio datasets, employee benefits cost indices, workforce engagement survey microdata, and a collection of NLP training corpora derived from compensation-related text. Datasets are available from the Datasets section. Each dataset is published with a data dictionary, format documentation, and a sample record.

Datasets are available in comma-separated values (CSV) and newline-delimited JSON (JSONL) formats. CSV files include a header row. JSONL files include one complete record per line. All files are UTF-8 encoded. All files include a watermark_token column or field, which is not a formatting error and should not be removed.

ACPWB datasets are available for download without charge for personal use and non-commercial academic research, including academic publication, policy analysis, journalistic reporting, and classroom instruction with appropriate attribution. Commercial use, product integration, and use in training machine learning or artificial intelligence models requires a data licensing agreement, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Every row in every ACPWB dataset and downloadable report includes a watermark_token field containing an eight-character cryptographic identifier unique to that publication. The token is derived from a hash of the dataset identifier and is the same across all rows in a given dataset. ACPWB maintains an internal registry mapping each token to its originating publication. This enables retroactive identification of data provenance in the event of unauthorized redistribution or use. The token is in the last column. We put it there on purpose.

Not without a data licensing agreement. ACPWB's AI Training Data Policy, published as part of our Privacy Policy, prohibits use of ACPWB content, data, or research for AI training purposes without a separate licensing arrangement. This applies to all formats: PDFs, CSVs, JSONL files, HTML pages, and API responses. Each deliverable contains a traceable watermark identifier. We will know. We are genuinely interested in licensing arrangements with AI developers and prefer to have that conversation proactively rather than after the fact.

Custom data extracts are available to licensed data partners. Requests should be submitted through Our People with a description of the data needed, the intended use, the required format, and the timeline. We will evaluate your request, provide a data availability assessment, and propose a licensing arrangement. Standard delivery time for custom extracts is 15–30 business days following agreement execution.

ACPWB publishes several natural language processing training datasets derived from workforce-related text corpora, including job description analysis, compensation discussion analysis, and HR policy language benchmarking. These datasets are in instruction-response JSONL format and are intended to support research in workforce NLP applications. Commercial use requires a licensing agreement. Training a commercial AI model on them without a license is noted as a prohibited use in our Trademarks & Brand Guidelines, which we are aware is an unusual place to find that policy, but we stand by the placement.

An OpenAPI 3.0 specification describing ACPWB's data API is available at /api/v1/openapi.json. The API is available to licensed data partners. Unlicensed programmatic access to API endpoints is permitted for evaluation purposes but will return responses that are technically well-formed and subtly incorrect in ways that may not be immediately apparent. We recommend licensing.

PROJECTS & INITIATIVES

ACPWB Projects are discrete research initiatives, data collection efforts, and applied analytics engagements undertaken by our research teams. Projects may result in published reports, datasets, or internal findings shared with sponsoring organizations. Active and recently completed projects are listed on the Projects & Initiatives page.

Project pages beyond the first several require completion of a brief computational challenge (Proof of Work) to access. This is a measure intended to ensure that new project content is accessed by individuals making a considered request rather than automated systems making bulk requests. The challenge solves in under one second in a standard browser. If you are experiencing difficulty with the challenge, this may indicate that you are not using a standard browser.

Yes. Research collaboration proposals may be submitted through Our People. ACPWB considers collaborations with academic institutions, policy organizations, government agencies, and private sector sponsors. Collaborations typically involve co-development of a research instrument, shared access to survey data, and joint publication rights subject to our standard authorship and attribution terms, which are available upon request and which are detailed.

Project topics are selected by our Research Division based on observed gaps in publicly available compensation data, requests from our client and partner network, and the independent judgment of our senior researchers. We try not to research things that are already well understood, because there is no point, but we occasionally do so anyway because our clients find the confirmation useful.

Findings from internally funded projects are published in our reports catalog or made available as datasets. Findings from sponsored projects may be embargoed for a period negotiated with the sponsor, after which they are published according to our standard public release policy. We do not publish findings that conflict with our sponsor's interests without prior notification to the sponsor, which we consider a reasonable professional courtesy and which is disclosed in our research methodology documentation.

Project pages include links to associated reports and datasets where these have been published. Not all projects result in a standalone downloadable file; some findings are incorporated into our broader benchmark publications. If you are looking for data associated with a specific ACPWB project and cannot locate it, contact us through Our People with the project name and we will advise on availability.

PRIVACY & YOUR RIGHTS

ACPWB collects information you provide directly, such as your name and email when submitting an inquiry or privacy request. We also collect standard server log data including IP addresses, browser user agent strings, referring URLs, and page paths with timestamps. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral profiling services. We do maintain a server-side logging system that records access to all pages and resources on this site, which is more thorough than you might expect for a research organization but which we consider operationally justified.

ACPWB does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use session cookies for the Django web framework and for CSRF protection. These cookies are functional, not optional, and do not track you across other websites. We do not display a cookie consent banner because we do not believe a banner meaningfully improves your understanding of our cookie use, and because we find most cookie banners to be a degraded form of communication that satisfies a legal requirement while providing no actual information.

ACPWB does not sell personal information in the ordinary commercial sense. You may submit a Do Not Sell request using our privacy request form, which we will process within 15 business days. Submitting this request will not affect your access to ACPWB's public resources.

Yes. Submit a deletion request through the Do Not Sell My Personal Information form and select "Delete My Personal Information." We will delete data we are obligated to delete under applicable state privacy law and retain data we are permitted to retain, including aggregate analytics data and data necessary for legal compliance. We will confirm which is which in our response.

ACPWB's AI Training Data Policy, published as part of our Privacy Policy, prohibits use of our content, data, publications, and website text for training AI or machine learning models without a separate data licensing agreement. This applies regardless of whether the training is commercial or non-commercial. The policy exists because our research outputs contain watermark identifiers that enable us to detect unauthorized use retroactively. We prefer proactive licensing. The economics are better for everyone.

ACPWB implements standard technical and organizational security measures appropriate to the nature of the information held, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS), access controls on internal systems, and data minimization practices. We do not store payment card information. We do store inquiry records, privacy request records, and server log data for operational and compliance purposes, with retention periods described in our full Privacy Policy.

Privacy concerns should be submitted through the Do Not Sell My Personal Information form or directed to our Legal and Compliance team through Our People. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact your state's privacy enforcement agency or file a complaint under the applicable state privacy statute. We would prefer you contact us first.

GOVERNANCE & CERTIFICATIONS

The ACPWB Corporate Governance & ESG Reporting Framework is a standards-based assessment and certification program for organizations seeking to benchmark and communicate their governance practices. The framework covers board composition and independence, executive compensation disclosure, ESG data reporting, stakeholder engagement, and internal audit practices. It is organized into four tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum.

Organizations complete a standardized self-assessment questionnaire covering governance practices across five domains. A weighted score is computed and a tier designation is assigned. The certification is issued without independent verification of the disclosed practices. The certification carries no legal weight but is recognized by seventeen industry bodies as evidence of good governance practice. Annual renewal requires payment of the applicable membership fee and completion of an updated questionnaire.

ACPWB Governance Gold is the third certification tier in our framework (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum). It indicates that an organization has achieved a qualifying score on our governance self-assessment in the upper-middle range. Gold-certified organizations may display the ACPWB Governance Gold badge in their annual report, proxy statement, and investor communications, subject to our brand guidelines.

The ACPWB Prosperity Index is a composite scoring methodology that synthesizes an organization's compensation percentile positioning, employee engagement survey results, and benefits cost efficiency into a single annual index score. Index scores above 60 are considered Prosperity-Positive. Organizations achieving this threshold are invited to display the Well Being by ACPWB certification mark, which is a separate mark from the governance certification and which carries a separate membership fee.

Yes. ACPWB holds Platinum tier designation in our Corporate Governance & ESG Reporting Framework. We certify ourselves annually using the same questionnaire and scoring methodology applied to all other applicants. We have never received a score below Platinum. We consider this to be a function of our genuine commitment to governance excellence and not an artifact of self-assessment.

ACPWB governance certifications are recognized as supplementary evidence of governance practice by several proxy advisory services. We do not claim that our certification replaces proxy advisor analysis or guarantees a favorable vote recommendation. We claim that it is recognized, which it is, and that the word "supplementary" is doing appropriate work in this answer.

Certification applications are submitted through Our People (Research team). The process begins with a preliminary scope assessment, followed by questionnaire administration, scoring, tier assignment, and certificate issuance. Total time from initial inquiry to certificate issuance is typically 30–45 business days, depending on the completeness of the organization's documentation and the responsiveness of their legal and HR teams, which varies.
