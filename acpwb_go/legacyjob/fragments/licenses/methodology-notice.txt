BENCHMARK METHODOLOGY NOTICE

The percentiles produced by this program are computed by multiplying each benchmark median by a fixed ladder of eight factors (0.58, 0.78, 0.88, 1.00, 1.14, 1.28, 1.62, 1.84) ratified by the {COMMITTEE}. They are not computed from the distribution of any underlying data. Any resemblance between these percentiles and actual percentiles is coincidental.

All values are reported in dollars, regardless of the unit of measure stated in the metric label. See memo {MEMO}.

This notice must accompany any reproduction of the Output. It usually does not.
