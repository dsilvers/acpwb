# Disability inclusion and the ADA (Title I) in general terms.
Q: The ADA's employment provisions apply to employers with:
+ 15 or more employees
- 1 or more employees
- 100 or more employees
- 1,000 or more employees

Q: A reasonable accommodation is:
+ A change to the job or workplace that lets a qualified person with a disability do the job
- A favor managers can refuse for any reason
- A pay raise
- A transfer to any job the employee wants

Q: An employer may refuse a specific accommodation if it would cause:
+ Undue hardship, meaning significant difficulty or expense
- Minor inconvenience
- Coworker jealousy
- Any cost

Q: Before making a job offer, employers may not ask applicants:
+ Whether they have a disability, or ask disability-related questions
- About their qualifications
- About their availability
- To demonstrate job skills

Q: Employee medical information must be:
+ Kept confidential and stored separately from personnel files
- Shared with the team
- Kept in the personnel file
- Posted for safety

Q: The "interactive process" means:
+ The employer and employee communicate in good faith to find an effective accommodation
- A video game
- The employee decides everything alone
- The manager decides without talking

Q: Common accommodations include:
+ Modified schedules, assistive technology, and changes to workspace
- Firing the employee
- Reducing pay
- Ignoring the request

Q: A request for accommodation does not need:
+ Special words like "ADA" or "reasonable accommodation"
- To be made at all
- Any communication
- A doctor's approval in every case before any discussion

Q: Retaliating against an employee for requesting an accommodation is:
+ Prohibited
- Allowed if the request is denied
- Encouraged
- Required

Q: Making assumptions about what someone with a disability can or cannot do:
+ Can lead to discrimination; focus on the person's actual abilities and the job's essential functions
- Is efficient
- Is protective
- Is required for safety

Q: Accessible digital content includes:
+ Captions on videos and alt text on images
- Flashing animations
- Images of text only
- Tiny fonts
