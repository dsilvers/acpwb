WHAT IS PERCH?

PERCH — the Annual Conference on Pay Equity & Remuneration Compensation — is the Midwest's premier gathering for compensation professionals, HR leaders, policy advocates, and researchers committed to building fairer, more transparent, and more accountable pay practices.

Hosted by the American Corporation for Public Well Being since its inaugural edition in 2014, PERCH brings together practitioners and researchers who share a conviction that compensation transparency and pay equity are not just ethical imperatives — they are organizational performance imperatives. Every session, every panel, and every conversation at PERCH is grounded in that conviction.

PERCH is named for the Great Lakes perch that forms the centerpiece of its beloved Annual PERCH Dinner — a Wisconsin fish fry tradition that has closed every Day 1 since 2014.

CONFERENCE HISTORY

PERCH was founded in 2014 by ACPWB's research division as a single-day symposium intended to convene thirty to forty Wisconsin-based compensation professionals. Demand exceeded expectations: 142 attendees registered for the inaugural conference at the Hilton Milwaukee City Center, and the conference has grown every year since.

FORMAT

Two Full Days

Thursday and Friday each year, in early fall. Full days of programming with multiple concurrent breakout tracks.

Keynote Speakers

Each conference features opening and closing keynotes from nationally recognized researchers, practitioners, and policy leaders.

Breakout Tracks

Four concurrent tracks across two rounds each day, covering technical, strategic, legal, and analytical dimensions of compensation.

The PERCH Dinner

A Wisconsin fish fry tradition held Thursday evening at a Milwaukee-area venue. Included with full conference registration since 2014.

WHO ATTENDS

PERCH draws a practitioner-research mix that distinguishes it from purely academic or purely HR conferences:

  • Compensation and total rewards professionals
  • Chief Human Resources Officers and CHROs
  • Employment attorneys and HR compliance counsel
  • Academic researchers in labor economics, organizational behavior, and employment law
  • Policy advocates and nonprofit leaders working on pay equity
  • Compensation technology and analytics vendors
  • Corporate governance professionals and compensation committee advisors

The PERCH Pay Equity Recognition Award

Awarded annually since PERCH 2015, the PERCH Pay Equity Recognition Award honors an organization that has achieved documented, measurable progress toward pay equity — and that is willing to share its methodology with the PERCH community. Nominees are accepted through the ACPWB research division. Past recipients include manufacturing firms, healthcare systems, technology companies, and public sector employers.
