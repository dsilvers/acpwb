# Research integrity for ACPWB reports.
Q: ACPWB research reports must:
+ Accurately describe methods, data sources, and limitations
- Hide limitations
- Invent data when needed
- Copy competitors' findings

Q: Fabricating survey responses is:
+ Research misconduct
- Data enrichment
- Allowed for small samples
- Required for deadlines

Q: Changing a chart's scale to exaggerate a small difference is:
+ Misleading
- Good design
- Required
- Harmless

Q: Data from a small or unrepresentative sample should be:
+ Presented with appropriate caution
- Presented as definitive
- Hidden
- Doubled

Q: Citing sources in reports:
+ Gives credit and lets readers verify claims
- Is optional
- Is plagiarism
- Slows reports

Q: Plagiarism includes:
+ Using others' words or ideas without credit
- Quoting with attribution
- Citing sources
- Summarizing your own work

Q: Corrections to published reports should be:
+ Made promptly and noted
- Hidden
- Ignored
- Delayed until next year

Q: Conflicts of interest in sponsored research must be:
+ Disclosed
- Hidden
- Ignored
- Celebrated

Q: Raw research data should be:
+ Retained according to policy so results can be verified
- Deleted after publishing
- Kept on a personal drive
- Shared publicly with personal data

Q: Statistical significance:
+ Does not by itself mean a result is important in practice
- Means a result is always true
- Means the sample is large
- Is optional decoration

Q: Each ACPWB report includes a Report ID because it:
+ Identifies the report and helps track copies
- Is decorative
- Counts pages
- Is the author's password
