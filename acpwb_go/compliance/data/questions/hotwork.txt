# Hot work. Answers follow NFPA 51B and OSHA 1910.252.
Q: Hot work includes:
+ Welding, cutting, brazing, soldering, and grinding that produces sparks or flame
- Working in a warm office
- Making coffee
- Sending urgent emails

Q: Before hot work outside a designated area, you need:
+ A hot work permit issued after the area is inspected
- A verbal OK from anyone nearby
- Nothing if the job is short
- A weather forecast

Q: Under NFPA 51B, combustible materials within how many feet of hot work must be removed or protected?
+ 35 feet
- 3 feet
- 100 yards
- 1 foot

Q: A fire watch must be maintained during hot work and for at least:
+ 60 minutes after the work ends, under NFPA 51B
- 5 minutes after the work ends
- Until lunch
- No time; fire watches are optional

Q: The person on fire watch must:
+ Have an extinguisher, know how to use it, and know how to sound the alarm
- Also be doing the welding
- Leave when the welder leaves
- Watch from another building

Q: If the sprinkler system is out of service in the work area, hot work:
+ Should not be done unless the permit authorizing individual approves special precautions
- Can proceed normally
- Should go faster
- Must be done at night

Q: Openings in walls and floors near hot work should be:
+ Covered to keep sparks from falling through
- Left open for ventilation
- Filled with paper
- Ignored

Q: Hot work on a drum that held flammable liquid requires:
+ It to be cleaned and tested or made safe before cutting or welding
- Nothing if it looks empty
- Opening the cap and lighting a match to check
- Tapping on it first

Q: Before hot work, nearby smoke detectors may be:
+ Temporarily covered only under the permit, then restored when work ends
- Removed permanently
- Painted over
- Unplugged forever

Q: Hot work permits must be:
+ Posted at the job site and closed out when the fire watch ends
- Kept in the office drawer
- Signed after the job
- Optional for contractors

Q: Contractors performing hot work at ACPWB sites:
+ Must follow the ACPWB hot work permit program
- Use their own rules only
- Are exempt
- Need no fire watch
