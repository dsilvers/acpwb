# Accounts receivable and collections conduct.
Q: When contacting a customer about an overdue invoice, staff should be:
+ Professional, accurate, and respectful
- Threatening, to speed things up
- Vague about the amount
- Anonymous

Q: Before sending an account to collections, staff should confirm:
+ The invoice is accurate, was delivered, and is not under dispute
- The customer's birthday
- That the customer has other vendors
- The sales rep's commission

Q: A customer disputes an invoice. You should:
+ Log the dispute and route it for review before pursuing payment
- Ignore the dispute and add late fees
- Cancel the invoice without approval
- Tell the customer disputes are not allowed

Q: Writing off a bad debt requires:
+ Approval under the delegation of authority and proper documentation
- Deleting the invoice from the system
- The collector's personal decision
- A coin flip

Q: Days sales outstanding (DSO) measures:
+ How long, on average, it takes to collect payment after a sale
- How many days the sales team takes off
- The number of open sales jobs
- Days since the last sales meeting

Q: Accepting a customer payment in cash should:
+ Follow cash handling procedures with a receipt and prompt deposit
- Be put in your desk drawer until Friday
- Be counted by one person with no record
- Be used for petty cash

Q: Customer bank and card details collected for payment must be:
+ Protected under PCI and privacy rules, never stored in spreadsheets or email
- Saved in a shared spreadsheet for convenience
- Written on sticky notes
- Shared in chat

Q: Offering a customer an unapproved discount to collect faster is:
+ Not allowed without authorization under the pricing policy
- Encouraged at quarter end
- A collector's personal choice
- Required by law

Q: A credit hold on a customer account means:
+ New orders are paused until the account is reviewed or brought current
- The customer receives free credit
- The account is closed forever
- Sales can ship anything they want

Q: Applying a customer's payment to the wrong invoice to improve aging reports is:
+ Falsifying records and prohibited
- Clever cash management
- Required at quarter end
- Fine if it is fixed next month

Q: Unapplied cash that cannot be matched to an invoice should be:
+ Researched and documented until it is applied or refunded
- Moved into miscellaneous income
- Kept by the collector
- Ignored forever
