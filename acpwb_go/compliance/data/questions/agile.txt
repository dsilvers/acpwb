# Agile ceremonies, stand-ups, and project delivery.

Q: A daily stand-up is meant to be:
+ A short check-in on progress, plans, and blockers
- A 90-minute status presentation
- A meeting where everyone must stand on chairs
- A performance review

Q: A "blocker" in a stand-up is:
+ Something preventing progress that needs help to resolve
- A coworker who talks too long
- A calendar conflict with lunch
- The {COMMITTEE}

Q: A sprint retrospective is a time to:
+ Reflect on what went well and what to improve
- Assign blame for missed work
- Plan the holiday party
- Review salaries

Q: Work-in-progress (WIP) limits on a Kanban board help to:
+ Keep the team focused on finishing work before starting more
- Limit how much work anyone may do in a week
- Track overtime
- Decorate the board

Q: "Scope creep" refers to:
+ Uncontrolled growth in a project's requirements
- A spooky coworker
- An expanding mold problem in the {PLACE}
- A new zoom feature

Q: A project's "definition of done" is:
+ The agreed criteria that work must meet to be considered complete
- When the deadline passes
- When the manager stops asking
- When {EVENT} is over

Q: Estimating work in "story points" measures:
+ Relative effort or complexity, not hours
- How interesting the story is
- The number of pages in a report
- Bonus points for the team

Q: The critical path in a project schedule is:
+ The longest sequence of dependent tasks, which sets the earliest finish date
- The path to the cafeteria
- The most expensive task
- The CEO's favorite task

Q: A RACI chart identifies who is:
+ Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed
- Ready, Available, Committed, and Inspired
- Racing to finish first
- Required to attend {EVENT}

Q: Marking a task "done" when it is not, to make the dashboard green, is:
+ Misreporting, which undermines decisions
- Agile optimism
- Required at quarter end
- Allowed if it will be done soon

Q: A "spike" in agile work is:
+ A short, time-boxed investigation to answer a question or reduce uncertainty
- A sudden increase in the coffee budget
- A pointed comment in a retrospective
- A type of fence

Q: Changing priorities in the middle of a sprint should be:
+ Rare and agreed with the team, since it disrupts planned work
- Daily
- Done without telling the team
- Required by {SYSTEM}
