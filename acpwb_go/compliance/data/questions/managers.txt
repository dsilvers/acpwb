# Manager responsibilities.

Q: A manager who receives a complaint of harassment from an employee must:
+ Report it to HR promptly, even if the employee asks them not to
- Keep it confidential and do nothing
- Investigate it alone
- Ask the accused to apologize and close it

Q: Managers should make employment decisions based on:
+ Job-related criteria, applied consistently
- Personal friendships
- Who laughs at their jokes
- Who attends {EVENT}

Q: A manager's offhand promise of a promotion can:
+ Create expectations and legal risk; avoid promises you cannot keep
- Be ignored later without consequence
- Be used to motivate anyone
- Be required by policy

Q: When an employee mentions a medical issue affecting their work, a manager should:
+ Refer them to HR to discuss leave or accommodation options
- Ask for their diagnosis
- Tell them to work through it
- Tell the team

Q: Managers must approve non-exempt employees' timesheets:
+ Accurately, without editing hours down to avoid overtime
- By reducing hours to meet budget
- Without looking
- Only at year end

Q: Retaliation by a manager can include:
+ Changing schedules, excluding from meetings, or poor reviews after a complaint
- Thanking an employee for reporting
- Following up on a complaint
- Approving PTO

Q: A manager learns an employee is planning to report a safety issue to OSHA. The manager should:
+ Not interfere; employees have a right to report to OSHA
- Discourage it
- Threaten them
- Report the employee first

Q: Managers who socialize with their team after work should:
+ Remember that their conduct still reflects on their role
- Act as if they are not managers
- Discuss other employees' performance
- Pay for everyone's drinks on a P-card

Q: A manager should document performance issues:
+ Promptly, factually, and consistently
- Only when planning to terminate
- With adjectives about personality
- In the team chat

Q: Delegating tasks effectively means:
+ Being clear about the expected outcome, authority, and deadline
- Assigning everything to the newest employee
- Delegating accountability for results
- Delegating the work and taking the credit
