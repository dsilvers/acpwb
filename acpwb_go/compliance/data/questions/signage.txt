# Safety signs, colors, and labels. Correct answers follow ANSI Z535 and
# OSHA signage conventions.

Q: A sign with the signal word "DANGER" indicates:
+ A hazard that will result in death or serious injury if not avoided
- A mild inconvenience
- A slippery floor that might be damp
- A meeting room in use

Q: A "WARNING" sign indicates:
+ A hazard that could result in death or serious injury

Q: A "CAUTION" sign indicates:
+ A hazard that could result in minor or moderate injury
- A guaranteed fatality
- A celebration
- A parking spot

Q: Green safety signs typically show:
+ Safety equipment and safe conditions, such as first aid stations and emergency exits
- Danger zones
- Fire extinguishers only
- Restricted areas

Q: Red is used for:
+ Fire protection equipment and "DANGER" signal words
- Coffee machines
- Break areas
- Recycling bins

Q: A "NOTICE" sign is used for:
+ Information not related to personal injury, such as policies or procedures
- Imminent hazards
- Fire exits
- Fire extinguishers
