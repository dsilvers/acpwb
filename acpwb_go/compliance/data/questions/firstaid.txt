# Basic first aid. Correct answers follow standard public guidance; keep them
# accurate.

Q: [A colleague|{NAME}] [cuts their hand badly on the paper cutter|is bleeding heavily from the forearm after a fall in the {PLACE}]. The FIRST step to control the bleeding is to:
+ Apply firm, direct pressure to the wound with a clean cloth or dressing
- Rinse the wound under the break room tap for {N} minutes
- Raise the issue at the next {COMMITTEE} meeting
- Apply ice and wait

Q: Firm pressure is not stopping life-threatening bleeding from an arm or leg. The next step, while waiting for help, is to:
+ Apply a tourniquet above the wound, if one is available and you know how
- Remove the dressing to check whether it has stopped
- Ask the person to walk to the {PLACE}
- Give the person something to eat

Q: [During|At] {EVENT}, [a colleague|{NAME2}] is choking, cannot speak or cough, and is clutching their throat. You should:
+ Call for help and give abdominal thrusts (and back blows, if trained), continuing until the object comes out or they become unresponsive
- Give them a glass of water
- Wait for them to stop on their own
- Ask whether they have completed a food-safety acknowledgement

Q: [A colleague|{NAME}] is coughing forcefully after something "went down the wrong way" but can still speak. You should:
+ Encourage them to keep coughing and stay with them
- Begin abdominal thrusts immediately
- Slap them on the back as hard as possible
- Leave them to recover privately

Q: [A colleague|{NAME2}] begins having a seizure [in the {PLACE}|during a meeting]. You should:
+ Clear the area, cushion their head, and time the seizure; never put anything in their mouth
- Hold them still until it stops
- Put a pen or wallet between their teeth
- Pour water on their face

Q: You should call 911 for a seizure if:
+ It lasts longer than 5 minutes, the person is injured, or another seizure follows
- It happens on a {WEEKDAY}
- The person asks you not to
- Only if they have not completed {COURSE}

Q: [A colleague|{NAME}] with a known severe allergy has trouble breathing after eating at {EVENT}. You should:
+ Call 911 and help them use their epinephrine auto-injector
- Offer an antacid
- Ask them to wait until the event ends
- Look up the catering contract

Q: [A colleague|{NAME2}] burns their hand on the {PLACE} coffee machine. The best first step is to:
+ Cool the burn under cool running water for at least 10 minutes
- Apply butter
- Apply ice directly to the skin
- Cover it tightly and keep working

Q: [A colleague|{NAME}] suddenly has a drooping face, arm weakness, and slurred speech. You should:
+ Call 911 immediately and note the time symptoms started
- Suggest they lie down for an hour
- Drive them home
- Give them aspirin and coffee

Q: [A colleague|{NAME2}] feels faint after standing in a long line at {EVENT}. You should:
+ Help them lie down, raise their legs if possible, and call for help if they do not recover quickly
- Have them stand and walk it off
- Give them a sugary drink while they are unconscious
- Splash them with cold water

Q: [The first aid kit|First aid kits] on each floor [is|are] located:
+ Where shown on the floor's emergency map, and should be restocked through Facilities after use
- In the {PLACE}, behind the vending machine
- At the Security Office in Suite 4040, Tuesdays only
- Wherever the last person left it

Q: [Someone falls down the stairwell|A colleague falls on the stairs near the {DOOR}] and complains of neck pain. You should:
+ Keep them still, call 911, and not move them unless they are in danger
- Help them up and walk them to the {PLACE}
