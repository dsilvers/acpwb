# Facilities requests and building rules.
Q: Repairs and changes to office space should be requested through:
+ A Facilities work order
- Taping a note to the wall
- Asking a friend with tools
- Fixing it yourself after hours

Q: Hanging heavy items on walls or moving furniture that is bolted down requires:
+ Facilities approval and installation
- Strong tape
- A coworker to hold it
- No approval if it is your desk

Q: A water leak in the ceiling should be:
+ Reported immediately, with the area kept clear and electronics protected
- Caught in a coffee mug for a week
- Ignored if it is small
- Reported only to your manager next month

Q: Blocking fire doors, sprinklers, or electrical panels with storage is:
+ Prohibited
- Allowed if the items are light
- Fine during moves
- Encouraged to save space

Q: Building keys and access cards issued by Facilities are:
+ Company property, tracked, and returned when no longer needed
- Gifts to keep forever
- Freely copied at the hardware store
- Lent to visitors

Q: Personal appliances such as mini-fridges and coffee makers at desks:
+ Need Facilities approval because of electrical and fire safety
- Are always allowed
- Must be plugged into extension cords
- Are required for morale

Q: A broken handrail on a stairway should be:
+ Reported right away and the stairway marked if needed
- Used carefully until someone fixes it
- Removed by the nearest employee
- Painted

Q: After-hours HVAC requests should go through:
+ The Facilities request process, because systems run on schedules
- Opening the windows
- Adjusting the thermostat with a screwdriver
- Unplugging the controller

Q: Contractors working in the building must:
+ Check in with Facilities and follow site safety rules
- Work unsupervised wherever they like
- Use any employee's badge
- Skip orientation

Q: Lost-and-found items should be:
+ Turned in to Security or reception
- Kept by whoever finds them
- Thrown away immediately
- Sold online

Q: A bad smell like gas or burning in the building should be:
+ Reported immediately; leave the area and follow emergency instructions if it is strong
- Covered with air freshener
- Ignored until lunch
- Investigated with a lighter
