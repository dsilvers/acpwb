# Electrical safety. Correct answers follow OSHA and NFPA guidance.

Q: A power cord with damaged insulation should be:
+ Taken out of service and reported
- Wrapped in tape and used
- Used only for small devices
- Plugged into a power strip instead

Q: Plugging one power strip into another, known as daisy-chaining, is:
+ Not allowed; it can overload circuits and cause fires
- Allowed if both are new
- Allowed for holiday lights
- The best way to add outlets

Q: The space in front of an electrical panel should be:
+ Kept clear, typically at least 36 inches, so the panel can be reached quickly
- Used for extra storage
- Blocked by a filing cabinet for safety
- Decorated for {EVENT}

Q: Ground-fault circuit interrupters (GFCIs) are especially important:
+ Near water, such as in kitchens, break rooms, and restrooms
- Only in server rooms
- Only outdoors in summer
- Never; they are optional

Q: Extension cords should be:
+ Used temporarily, not as permanent wiring, and never run under rugs or through doorways
- Run under carpets to hide them
- Stapled to walls
- Connected end to end across the office

Q: If a coworker is being shocked by an electrical source, you should:
+ Not touch them; cut the power if you can do so safely and call 911
- Pull them away with your hands
- Throw water on them
- Wait for the shock to stop

Q: Only qualified, authorized people may:
+ Open electrical panels or work on electrical equipment
- Plug in a laptop
- Change a desk lamp bulb
- Use a power strip

Q: A space heater should be:
+ Approved by Facilities and plugged directly into a wall outlet, away from paper and fabric
- Plugged into a power strip under the desk
- Used to dry wet coats
- Left on overnight

Q: A burning smell from an outlet should be:
+ Reported to Facilities immediately, and the equipment unplugged if safe
- Covered with air freshener
- Ignored if it goes away
- Reported at the next {EVENT}

Q: Overhead power lines near a work area should be:
+ Avoided by at least 10 feet, which can include ladders and equipment
- Touched to test if they are live
- Used to hang decorations
- Avoided only if they spark
