# Outside employment and side businesses.
Q: Before taking a second job or starting a side business, an ACPWB employee should:
+ Review the outside activities policy and disclose it if required
- Keep it secret
- Ask a competitor for advice
- Quit their ACPWB job

Q: A side job is likely a conflict of interest when it:
+ Competes with ACPWB or involves an ACPWB vendor or customer
- Is on weekends
- Involves teaching yoga
- Pays less than ACPWB

Q: Using ACPWB equipment or time for a side business is:
+ Not allowed
- Fine if you are fast
- Allowed on Fridays
- Encouraged for entrepreneurship

Q: Using confidential ACPWB information in a side job is:
+ Prohibited
- Allowed if you are paid less
- Fine after a year
- Encouraged

Q: Doing outside consulting for an ACPWB competitor is:
+ Generally not allowed and must be disclosed
- Fine if it is part-time
- Fine if it is at night
- Required for learning

Q: Soliciting coworkers to buy products from your side business at work:
+ Is limited by the solicitation policy
- Is always allowed
- Is required for team building
- Can be done through the company email list

Q: A second job that leaves you too tired to work safely:
+ Raises safety concerns that should be discussed with your manager
- Is your secret
- Is never a concern
- Improves performance

Q: Writing a book on your own time about general industry topics:
+ May be fine, but confidential information and company IP must be protected
- Is always prohibited
- Belongs to ACPWB automatically
- Requires using ACPWB's name

Q: Serving on the board of a for-profit company:
+ Requires disclosure and approval under the policy
- Never needs approval
- Is required for executives
- Must be kept secret

Q: Volunteer work for a nonprofit is:
+ Usually encouraged, as long as it does not create a conflict
- Always a conflict
- Prohibited
- Required

Q: If you are unsure whether an outside activity is a conflict, you should:
+ Ask Ethics or your manager before starting
- Start and see if anyone notices
- Ask the outside business
- Search online
