# Student loan benefits. Answers follow SECURE 2.0 and Section 127.
Q: Under SECURE 2.0, employers may make retirement plan matching contributions based on:
+ Employees' qualified student loan payments
- Car payments
- Credit card payments
- Rent

Q: Section 127 educational assistance can include tax-free employer payments toward:
+ Qualified education loans, within the annual $5,250 limit
- Mortgages
- Any loan
- Gambling debts

Q: Employees who want the student loan match must usually:
+ Certify their qualified loan payments
- Do nothing
- Quit
- Pay the employer

Q: Student loan repayment benefits are:
+ Provided as described in the plan and policy
- Unlimited
- Paid in stock
- Secret

Q: Sharing your student loan account password with the benefits vendor:
+ Should only be done through the vendor's secure process if required
- Should be emailed
- Should be posted
- Is required by phone

Q: Public Service Loan Forgiveness:
+ Is a federal program for qualifying public service employment, not most private employers
- Applies to all employers
- Is an ACPWB benefit
- Is a tax form

Q: Student loan benefit questions should go to:
+ HR or the benefits provider
- Coworkers
- Social media
- Nobody

Q: Messages offering to "forgive" student loans for an upfront fee are often:
+ Scams
- Government programs
- ACPWB benefits
- Required

Q: Financial wellness resources can help employees:
+ Plan repayment and budget
- Avoid all debt instantly
- Skip payments
- Nothing

Q: Student loan matching contributions go:
+ Into the employee's retirement plan account
- To the lender
- To the manager
- To charity
