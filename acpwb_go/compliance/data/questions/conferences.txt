# Attending conferences and industry events (including the PERCH Conference).
Q: Before registering for an external conference, you need:
+ Manager approval and a booking through the approved travel process
- Only a credit card
- Approval from the conference organizer
- Nothing; conferences are free

Q: Your conference badge should be:
+ Worn while at the event and removed when you leave the venue
- Worn on the train home for networking
- Lent to a friend for the afternoon sessions
- Left on the hotel bar

Q: Talking about confidential ACPWB projects at a conference reception is:
+ Not allowed; assume conversations are overheard
- Allowed after the second drink
- Fine if the listener seems nice
- Encouraged for networking

Q: A vendor at the conference offers you an expensive gift. You should:
+ Follow the gift policy, which may require declining or reporting it
- Accept and say nothing
- Ask for a second one
- Trade it for a better gift

Q: Presenting at a conference on behalf of ACPWB requires:
+ Approval of the slides by Communications, and Legal where needed
- No approval if you are a speaker
- Using the competitor's template
- Sharing your password with the AV team

Q: Conduct at a conference is governed by:
+ ACPWB's code of conduct and the event's code of conduct
- Nothing; you are off duty
- The hotel's pool rules only
- Whatever the keynote speaker says

Q: Public Wi-Fi at a conference venue should be used:
+ With the company VPN, and never for sensitive work without it
- For everything, because it is free
- To share files with strangers
- To test your company password

Q: Conference expenses should be submitted:
+ With itemized receipts and the business purpose
- As one lump sum labeled "conference"
- Only for meals you enjoyed
- With other attendees' receipts

Q: Collecting competitors' confidential information at a conference is:
+ Not allowed; use only public information obtained ethically
- Encouraged if you find a lost laptop
- Fine if you pretend to be a customer
- Part of competitive intelligence

Q: Harassment at a conference reception should be:
+ Reported to the event organizer and to ACPWB HR
- Ignored because it was not in the office
- Laughed off
- Posted on social media first

Q: After attending a conference, you should:
+ Share useful takeaways with your team
- Keep everything you learned secret
- Request a second conference immediately
- Delete your notes
