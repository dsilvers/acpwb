# Cloud cost management (FinOps).
Q: The fastest way to cut waste in a cloud account is usually to:
+ Shut down idle resources and right-size oversized ones
- Buy more reserved capacity for everything
- Turn off billing alerts
- Move every workload to the most expensive region

Q: Every cloud resource should be tagged with:
+ An owner and a cost center, so spending can be traced
- The engineer's favorite movie
- Nothing; tags slow things down
- The word "temporary"

Q: A budget alert in the cloud console is meant to:
+ Warn owners before spending exceeds the plan
- Shut down production automatically without warning
- Email the CFO a congratulations card
- Hide charges until the end of the year

Q: Development environments left running overnight and on weekends:
+ Should be scheduled to stop when not in use
- Must run 24/7 for morale
- Are free on weekends
- Improve uptime statistics

Q: Data transfer out of a cloud provider:
+ Can carry significant charges and should be planned
- Is always free
- Is billed only on leap years
- Is paid by the receiving website

Q: Reserved or committed-use discounts make sense when:
+ A workload's usage is steady and predictable
- A project might be cancelled next week
- The team wants to spend the remaining budget
- No one knows what the workload does

Q: An unexpected spike on the cloud bill should be:
+ Investigated quickly, because it can signal a mistake or a compromised account
- Ignored until the annual review
- Paid without questions
- Split across several cost centers

Q: Old snapshots and backups that are past their retention period should be:
+ Deleted under the retention schedule, unless on legal hold
- Kept forever "just in case"
- Copied to a second region
- Renamed

Q: Who is responsible for a cloud resource's cost?
+ The team that owns it, as shown by its tags
- The cloud provider
- Whoever noticed the bill
- Nobody

Q: Giant virtual machines created "to be safe" are:
+ A common source of waste; start small and scale with evidence
- Required by policy
- Free if they are idle
- The fastest way to save money

Q: Cryptocurrency mining software appears on a company cloud server. This most likely means:
+ The account has been compromised and must be reported to Security
- An engineer found a creative cost-saving idea
- The provider is testing new features
- The server is helping with the budget
