# Making ethical decisions.
Q: A quick ethics check before acting is:
+ Would I be comfortable if this appeared in the news or was explained to my family?
- Will anyone notice?
- Is it profitable today?
- Did someone tell me to?

Q: When the right course of action is unclear, you should:
+ Ask for guidance from your manager, Legal, or Ethics
- Do what is easiest
- Wait until it becomes someone else's problem
- Flip a coin

Q: "Everyone does it" is:
+ Not a justification for unethical behavior
- A valid defense
- Company policy
- A legal standard

Q: Pressure to meet a target does not justify:
+ Cutting corners on laws or policies
- Working efficiently
- Asking for help
- Prioritizing tasks

Q: When laws and company policy differ, employees should follow:
+ The stricter standard
- Whichever is easier
- The one their manager prefers
- Neither

Q: An ethical dilemma is:
+ A situation where values or obligations conflict and the right answer is not obvious
- A clear policy violation
- A scheduling conflict
- A tax form

Q: Good ethical decisions consider:
+ Laws, policies, values, and the impact on others
- Only short-term profit
- Only personal benefit
- Only speed

Q: If you realize you made an unethical decision, you should:
+ Report it and help correct it
- Hide it
- Blame someone else
- Hope it is forgotten

Q: Small ethical shortcuts:
+ Can grow into bigger problems over time
- Do not matter
- Build efficiency
- Are encouraged

Q: Leaders set the ethical tone by:
+ Modeling the behavior they expect and responding fairly to concerns
- Making exceptions for themselves
- Ignoring problems
- Punishing people who ask questions

Q: Documenting the reasoning behind a difficult decision helps because:
+ It shows the factors considered and supports accountability
- It slows decisions
- It is never useful
- It hides mistakes
