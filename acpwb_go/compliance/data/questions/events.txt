# Planning and running company events.
Q: A company event with outside guests needs:
+ An approved budget, a venue that meets capacity and accessibility needs, and a safety plan
- Only a playlist
- A guest list kept secret from Security
- Nothing if it is off-site

Q: Venue capacity limits posted by the fire marshal are:
+ Legal maximums that must not be exceeded
- Suggestions for small groups
- Negotiable with the venue manager
- Only for weekends

Q: Event registration forms should collect:
+ Only the information needed to run the event
- Social Security numbers for name tags
- Home addresses of every guest's family
- Passwords to speed up check-in

Q: Accessibility at events includes:
+ Step-free access, accessible restrooms, and accommodations people request
- Only a ramp to the stage
- Nothing unless someone complains
- A sign that says "accessible"

Q: Inviting government officials to a company event requires:
+ Review by Legal or Ethics under the anti-bribery and gift rules
- Nothing; they are guests
- A bigger gift bag
- A separate VIP bar

Q: A guest becomes visibly intoxicated at a company event. Staff should:
+ Stop serving them and help arrange safe transportation
- Serve one more for the road
- Ask them to drive others home
- Take photos for the newsletter

Q: An event emergency plan should include:
+ Exits, an assembly point, first aid, and who calls 911
- Only the menu
- The DJ's phone number
- A list of sponsors

Q: Photos taken at company events and used publicly need:
+ Notice to attendees and consent where required
- No consent ever
- Only the photographer's approval
- A watermark only

Q: Event vendors must be:
+ Engaged through Procurement with a contract and insurance
- Paid in cash at the door
- Friends of the organizer
- Chosen by coin toss

Q: Raffle prizes at company events must:
+ Follow the gaming laws and company policy, with Legal review if needed
- Be purchased with petty cash without records
- Go to the organizer first
- Be cash only

Q: Harassment at a company event is:
+ Covered by the harassment policy just like at the office
- Excused because it is a party
- Handled by the DJ
- Allowed after 9 p.m.
