# Home offices and working from somewhere else.

Q: A home office should have:
+ A clear path, safe electrical setup, and a working smoke detector
- Extension cords running across the doorway
- A space heater under the desk near paper
- A desk in the bathtub

Q: Confidential calls from home should be taken:
+ Where others in the household cannot overhear
- On speaker in the kitchen
- On the balcony with neighbors nearby
- With smart speakers listening

Q: A "workation" in another state may:
+ Affect payroll taxes and require approval before you go
- Never matter
- Be kept secret from HR
- Count as a vacation day while working

Q: Printing confidential documents at home should be:
+ Avoided where possible, and documents shredded securely when no longer needed
- Done freely and recycled with household paper
- Done at a copy shop
- Left on the kitchen table

Q: A work injury at your home office during working hours:
+ Should be reported like any other work injury
- Is never covered
- Should be hidden
- Is the landlord's fault

Q: Family members using your ACPWB laptop is:
+ Not allowed
- Allowed for homework
- Allowed for games after hours
- Allowed if they log in as you

Q: Your remote work address must be:
+ Kept current in the HR system
- A secret
- The same as the office
- Changed weekly

Q: Working from a café on a laptop, you should:
+ Use a privacy screen, the VPN, and never leave the laptop unattended
- Ask a stranger to watch your laptop
- Leave it while you order
- Share your screen with nearby tables

Q: Background noise from children or pets during calls is:
+ Understandable; mute when you are not speaking
- A policy violation
- Required for authenticity
- Recorded in your review

Q: A home office ergonomic stipend can be used for:
+ Eligible items such as a chair, monitor, or keyboard, per the policy
- A new television
- A vacation
- A treadmill for the dog
