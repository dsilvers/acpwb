+ Have a business purpose and stay within policy limits

- Be paid in cash

+ Pre-approval from Compliance
- Nothing special
- A nicer restaurant
- A gift bag

+ Must follow the gifts and entertainment policy, and ACPWB staff should usually attend

- Should be resold

- Allowed if the client insists

- Letting them explore
- A tour of the server room

+ The names of attendees and the business purpose
- Only the total
- The tip only
