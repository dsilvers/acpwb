+ Can trap heat and increase fire risk
- Charges them faster
- Is recommended

Q: Lithium batteries shipped by the company must:
+ Follow dangerous goods shipping rules
- Be labeled "batteries" by hand only

Q: Storage of many lithium batteries in one place should be:
+ Planned with Facilities and EHS for fire safety
- In a cardboard box under a desk
- Next to the heater

+ Should be inspected and taken out of service if damaged
