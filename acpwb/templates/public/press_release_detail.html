About ACPWB

The American Corporation for Public Well Being is a Milwaukee-based workforce research and advocacy organization founded in 2006. ACPWB produces compensation benchmarking studies, workforce equity analyses, and governance research used by HR professionals and organizational leaders across the United States. The organization's research catalog, datasets, and publications are available at acpwb.com.
