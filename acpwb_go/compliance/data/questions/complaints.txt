# Customer complaint handling.
Q: The first step in handling a customer complaint is to:
+ Listen, acknowledge the issue, and record it
- Argue about whose fault it is
- Transfer the customer to a random department
- Promise a full refund immediately

Q: Every complaint should be logged so that:
+ Trends can be spotted and root causes fixed
- Customers can be blacklisted
- Agents can be graded on volume only
- Nothing happens

Q: A complaint that alleges a safety problem with a product must be:
+ Escalated immediately under the product safety procedure
- Treated like any billing question
- Closed if the customer is rude
- Answered with a coupon

Q: A complaint about discrimination by an ACPWB employee should be:
+ Escalated to HR or Legal promptly
- Handled informally by the agent
- Deleted to protect the employee
- Posted on the team board

Q: Promising a customer an outcome you cannot authorize:
+ Creates bigger problems; explain what you can do and escalate
- Is great customer service
- Is required to close the ticket
- Is fine if it ends the call

Q: When a complaint is resolved, you should:
+ Confirm the resolution with the customer and close the record
- Close the ticket before telling the customer
- Reopen it for metrics
- Forward it to everyone

Q: A customer posts a complaint publicly on social media. You should:
+ Follow the social media response guidelines or route it to the social team
- Reply angrily from your personal account
- Post the customer's account details publicly
- Report the customer as spam

Q: Root cause analysis of complaints helps to:
+ Fix the underlying issue so it does not happen again
- Assign blame
- Increase complaint volume
- Close tickets faster without fixing anything

Q: Personal data a customer shares in a complaint should be:
+ Used only to resolve the complaint and protected like other customer data
- Shared with marketing for upselling
- Posted in the knowledge base
- Printed for the break room

Q: A customer is abusive or threatening on the phone. You may:
+ Follow the procedure to end the call politely and escalate to a supervisor or Security
- Respond with matching insults
- Keep listening indefinitely
- Hang up and delete the record

Q: The time limit for acknowledging a written complaint is set by:
+ The customer service policy and any applicable regulations
- The mood of the agent
- The complaint's word count
- Nothing; acknowledgment is optional
