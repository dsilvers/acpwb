# Insurance, certificates of insurance, and claims.

Q: Vendors performing work on ACPWB premises are often required to provide:
+ A certificate of insurance showing required coverage
- A gift basket
- A copy of their passport
- Proof of {EVENT} invitation

Q: Damage to ACPWB property caused by a contractor should be:
+ Documented and reported to Facilities and Risk Management
- Fixed by the employee who saw it
- Ignored
- Paid in cash by the contractor to you

Q: Using a personal vehicle for business generally means:
+ Your personal auto insurance is primary; check the policy and the travel policy
- ACPWB insurance covers everything
- No insurance is needed
- Your manager's insurance applies

Q: Rental car insurance on business trips should be:
+ Purchased or declined according to the travel policy
- Always declined
- Always purchased at the highest level
- Billed to your personal card only

Q: An incident that might lead to a claim against ACPWB should be:
+ Reported promptly to Legal or Risk Management
- Kept quiet
- Discussed on social media
- Settled personally

Q: Admitting fault at the scene of a car accident while on company business is:
+ Something to avoid; exchange information and report it
- Required by policy
- A courtesy
- Legally required in all cases

Q: Business travel accident insurance generally covers:
+ Specified accidents during business travel, per the policy
- Lost luggage only
- Bad meals
- Missed flights due to oversleeping

Q: Personal belongings at the office damaged by a leak are:
+ Generally not covered by ACPWB insurance; check your own renters or homeowners policy
- Always reimbursed
- Covered at double value
- Covered if expensive
