# Giving and receiving feedback.
Q: Effective feedback is:
+ Specific, timely, and about behavior and impact
- Vague
- Months late
- About personality

Q: A useful feedback model is:
+ Situation, Behavior, Impact
- Shout, Blame, Ignore
- Wait, Forget, Complain
- Praise, Criticize, Leave

Q: Receiving feedback well means:
+ Listening, asking questions, and considering it
- Arguing immediately
- Ignoring it
- Retaliating

Q: Positive feedback should be:
+ Specific about what went well
- Generic
- Rare
- Secret

Q: Feedback in public vs. private:
+ Praise can be public; critical feedback is usually best in private
- Criticism should be public
- All feedback must be private
- Feedback should never be given

Q: 360 feedback collects input from:
+ Managers, peers, direct reports, and sometimes customers
- Only the manager
- Only friends
- Only HR

Q: Anonymous peer feedback should be:
+ Constructive and job-related
- Personal attacks
- Gossip
- About protected characteristics

Q: Feedback that references someone's age, race, or gender is:
+ Inappropriate and potentially discriminatory
- Helpful
- Required
- Neutral

Q: Waiting for the annual review to share a problem:
+ Denies the person a chance to improve sooner
- Is best practice
- Is required
- Is kind

Q: Upward feedback to your manager should be:
+ Respectful and specific
- Avoided completely
- Anonymous only
- Hostile
