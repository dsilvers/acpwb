# Contracts and ethical negotiation.

+ Required reviews are complete and the signer has authority
- The other side signs first
- It is printed in color

Q: Negotiating with a vendor, it is appropriate to:
+ Push for better terms honestly
- Lie about a competing offer that does not exist
- Threaten the vendor's personal reputation
- Promise future business you cannot guarantee

Q: Automatic renewal clauses in contracts should be:
+ Tracked so the contract can be renewed or cancelled on purpose
- Ignored
- Removed by hand after signing
- Hidden from Finance

Q: Starting work with a vendor before the contract is signed is:
+ Generally not allowed, because terms like confidentiality and liability are not yet in place
- Encouraged to save time
- Allowed for friends
- Required for urgent projects

Q: Indemnification clauses:
+ Allocate responsibility for certain losses and should be reviewed by Legal
- Are decorative
- Mean the vendor pays for everything
- Are only for insurance companies

Q: A vendor contract that allows them to use ACPWB data to train their AI models:
+ Requires review and may need to be negotiated out
- Is standard and harmless
- Is required
- Is good for the vendor relationship
