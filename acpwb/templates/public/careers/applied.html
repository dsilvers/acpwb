APPLICATION RECEIVED

Thank you for applying to the American Corporation for Public Well Being.

Application Logged

Your Application for {{ job.title }} Has Been Received.

Your materials have been received and logged in our applicant tracking system. A member of our talent acquisition team will review your application in the order it was received, which is to say: when they get to it.

If your qualifications are a strong match for this role, we will reach out to schedule an initial conversation. If they are not, we will reach out to let you know, or we will not reach out, depending on our current response-rate commitments. We are working to improve those commitments.

What Happens Next
  1. Your application is reviewed by a member of our talent team, typically within 5–10 business days of submission.
  2. Candidates selected for further consideration are contacted to schedule an introductory call.
  3. If selected after the introductory call, you will be invited to a technical interview with the hiring manager.
  4. Finalists participate in a panel interview and may be asked to complete a brief analytical exercise.
  5. Offers are extended to selected candidates, who then have five business days to respond. We will send a follow-up email on day four.
A Note from Our Talent Team

We review every application with care and intention. This is true. We also review approximately several hundred applications per open role, which affects the pace at which care and intention can be applied. We appreciate your patience and your understanding of the fundamental tension between those two facts.

ACPWB does not use automated rejection algorithms. Every application is seen by a human being who is doing their best. We think that matters and we think you deserve to know it.
