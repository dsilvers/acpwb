THE TRADITION

Every year since PERCH's inaugural 2014 conference, the conference has closed the same way: a Wisconsin fish fry dinner on Friday night, a Brandy Old Fashioned in hand, and two days' worth of conversations continuing well into the evening.

The PERCH Dinner began as a simple gathering at the Hilton Milwaukee's Monarch Room in 2014. What no one anticipated was how central it would become to the conference experience. Year after year, attendees cite the dinner as the moment when the conference shifts from professional development to genuine community — when the conversations stop being about pay equity and start being about everything else that matters.

In 2021, when PERCH went virtual, we mailed fish fry kits and Old Fashioned ingredient boxes to all full-conference registrants. It was, by general consensus, a noble attempt that only deepened everyone's appreciation for the real thing.

THE MENU

The PERCH Dinner menu is a Wisconsin fish fry. This is not negotiable.

Lake Perch

The namesake of the conference itself. Lightly breaded, pan-fried lake perch. The non-negotiable centerpiece of every PERCH Dinner since 2014.

Coleslaw

Wisconsin-style creamy coleslaw. Made from scratch at every venue we've worked with. A point of significant behind-the-scenes negotiation each year.

Potato Pancakes

Served with sour cream and applesauce. The dish that generates the most opinionated commentary at the dinner table, every year without fail.

Dark Rye Bread

From a local Milwaukee bakery. Served with butter. Simple. Correct.

Vegetarian Option

A cheese-forward vegetarian entrée available upon request at registration. Please note your preference during registration.

Dessert

Varies by venue. Past years have featured Door County cherry pie, kringle from a Racine bakery, and, memorably in 2018, a cheese course that went significantly over budget.

THE SIGNATURE DRINK

The Brandy Old Fashioned

The Brandy Old Fashioned is, indisputably, the state cocktail of Wisconsin. Made with brandy (not bourbon — this is Wisconsin), muddled fruit, a sugar cube, and bitters. Served sweet (with lemon-lime soda) or sour (with sour mix). Asked "sweet or sour?" at the PERCH Dinner bar is, for many attendees, the closest the conference gets to a political test.

The PERCH Dinner bar opens at 5:30 PM for the pre-dinner reception. The first round of Old Fashioneds is on ACPWB.

Non-alcoholic Old Fashioned

Also available at the bar, prepared with the same muddled fruit and bitters — just without the brandy. It is, in our opinion, quite good and in no way a lesser choice.

The Brandy Old Fashioned Recipe
  • 1½ oz Wisconsin brandy
  • 1 sugar cube (or ½ tsp sugar)
  • 2–3 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 1 orange slice, muddled
  • 1 maraschino cherry, muddled
  • Splash of lemon-lime soda (sweet)
    or sour mix (sour)
  • Served on the rocks
