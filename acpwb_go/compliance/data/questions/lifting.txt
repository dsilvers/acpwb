# Manual material handling. Answers follow NIOSH and OSHA guidance.
Q: When lifting a box from the floor, you should:
+ Bend at the knees, keep your back straight, and hold the load close
- Bend at the waist with straight legs
- Hold the box at arm's length
- Twist while lifting to save time

Q: The NIOSH lifting equation's load constant, the maximum recommended weight under ideal conditions, is:
+ 51 pounds
- 10 pounds
- 100 pounds
- 200 pounds

Q: Twisting your body while carrying a load:
+ Increases the risk of back injury; turn with your feet instead
- Is safer than turning your feet
- Strengthens the back
- Is required for speed

Q: Before lifting, you should:
+ Test the load's weight and plan the route
- Lift first and plan later
- Close your eyes
- Hold your breath for the whole trip

Q: Heavy or awkward items should be moved:
+ With a team lift or mechanical aids such as carts and hand trucks
- By the strongest person alone
- By dragging them across the floor
- By throwing them

Q: Holding a load away from your body:
+ Greatly increases the strain on your lower back
- Reduces strain
- Has no effect
- Is required for balance

Q: When carrying a load with a partner, you should:
+ Agree on signals and move together
- Let one person decide without warning
- Lift at different times
- Walk at different speeds

Q: Lifting items from above shoulder height:
+ Is riskier; use a step stool or platform to bring the load closer
- Is safer than lifting from the floor
- Should be done quickly
- Is required for tall people

Q: Pain in the back after lifting at work should be:
+ Reported promptly
- Ignored until it gets worse
- Treated with more lifting
- Kept secret

Q: Back belts:
+ Are not proven to prevent back injuries, and safe lifting technique is still required
- Make any lift safe
- Replace training
- Should be worn tightly all day

Q: Storing heavy items on high shelves:
+ Should be avoided; keep heavy items between knee and shoulder height
- Is best practice
- Saves the most space safely
- Is required by Facilities
