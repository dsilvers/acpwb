# Office moves, desk relocations, and packing.

Q: Before an office move, confidential papers should be:
+ Shredded if no longer needed, or packed in locked crates
- Thrown in open boxes
- Left on the floor
- Taken home

Q: Moving crates should be labeled with:
+ Your name and new location, not with "Confidential HR Files"
- The contents in large letters
- Your password
- Nothing

Q: Lifting heavy items during a move should be:
+ Left to the movers, or done with proper lifting techniques and help
- Done by twisting quickly
- Done alone to save time
- Done on a chair

Q: IT equipment should be moved by:
+ IT or the authorized movers, following the move plan
- Anyone with a cart
- Visitors
- Throwing it

Q: Personal items left behind after a move may be:
+ Disposed of after a stated period
- Kept forever
- Shipped to your home free
- Given to {VENDOR}

Q: Unused furniture after a move should be:
+ Handled by Facilities through approved recycling or donation
- Left in the hallway
- Taken home without approval
- Burned

Q: Badge access after a move should be:
+ Updated for the new location and removed for the old one
- Kept for both forever
- Shared
- Disabled entirely

Q: Moving day is a good time to:
+ Clear out files that have passed their retention period, following the schedule
- Delete everything
- Keep everything
- Shred documents on legal hold
