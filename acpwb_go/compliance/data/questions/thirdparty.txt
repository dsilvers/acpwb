# Third-party and vendor risk management.

Q: Before engaging a new vendor that will handle ACPWB data, you should:
+ Complete the third-party risk assessment, including security and privacy review
- Sign the contract and assess later
- Ask the vendor if they are secure
- Choose the vendor with the best logo

Q: A vendor's sub-contractor will access ACPWB data. This should be:
+ Disclosed and covered by the contract and risk assessment
- Ignored, since it is the vendor's business
- Allowed only on weekends
- Kept confidential from Procurement

Q: Paying a vendor without a purchase order or contract:
+ Bypasses controls and is generally not allowed
- Is faster and preferred
- Is allowed for friends
- Is required for urgent requests

Q: A vendor emails new bank details and asks you to update their payment information. You should:
+ Verify the change by calling the vendor using a phone number already on file
- Update it immediately
- Reply to the email to confirm
- Call the number in the email signature

Q: Vendor performance should be:
+ Monitored against contract terms and service levels
- Assumed to be good
- Reviewed only if the vendor complains
- Measured by how many gifts they send

Q: When a vendor relationship ends, ACPWB should:
+ Remove the vendor's access and confirm the return or destruction of ACPWB data
- Leave access in place in case they return
- Delete the contract
- Send a farewell cake

Q: Sole-source purchases, without competitive bidding:
+ Require documented justification and approval
- Are always prohibited
- Are always preferred
- Are allowed if the vendor is local

Q: Fourth parties are:
+ Your vendor's vendors, which can also create risk
- The fourth vendor you hire
- Fourth-floor vendors
- Imaginary

Q: Vendor access to ACPWB systems should be:
+ Limited, monitored, and removed when no longer needed
- Permanent
- Shared accounts
- Administrator by default

Q: A vendor's data breach involving ACPWB data should be:
+ Reported to ACPWB promptly under the contract
- Kept secret
- Reported next year
- Posted publicly first
