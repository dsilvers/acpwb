# Shared kitchen, coffee, and break room etiquette.

Q: If you take the last cup of coffee, you should:
+ Start a new pot, or let Facilities know if supplies are out
- Leave the empty pot on the burner
- Hide the evidence
- Blame the {COMMITTEE}

Q: An empty coffee pot left on a hot burner can:
+ Crack and create a fire hazard
- Make better coffee
- Clean itself
- Set off the sprinkler as designed

Q: Dishes in the shared sink should be:
+ Washed and put away, or placed in the dishwasher
- Left to soak until Friday
- Left for Facilities
- Stacked artistically

Q: Food in the shared refrigerator should be:
+ Labeled with your name and date and removed before the scheduled cleanout
- Left indefinitely
- Shared with anyone who opens the door
- Stored in unlabeled containers to keep it mysterious

Q: Taking a coworker's labeled lunch from the fridge is:
+ Not allowed
- Allowed if it looks good
- Allowed on {WEEKDAY}s
- Allowed if you leave a thank-you note

Q: Microwaving popcorn should be done:
+ Following the package directions, while staying nearby
- On the highest setting for as long as possible
- While you go to a meeting
- Twice for extra crunch

Q: Spills on the break room floor should be:
+ Cleaned up or reported right away to prevent slips
- Left to evaporate
- Covered with paper towels and left
- Reported at {EVENT}

Q: The office coffee fund is:
+ Voluntary, and its money should be handled transparently
- Mandatory
- A way to fund the holiday party bar
- Managed by {VENDOR}

Q: Using the shared kettle to boil eggs is:
+ Not allowed; it is for water only
- A great time-saver
- Allowed if you clean it
- Required on Easter

Q: Leaving a sign that says "YOUR MOTHER DOESN'T WORK HERE" in the kitchen is:
+ Not the most professional way to encourage tidiness
- Required by policy
- A legal notice
- Inspirational

Q: Single-serve coffee pods should go in:
+ The bin listed on the posted recycling guidance
- The paper recycling bin with the lids on
- The sink
- The shredder

Q: A kitchen appliance that sparks or smells like burning should be:
+ Unplugged if safe, taken out of use, and reported to Facilities
- Used until it stops working
- Fixed with a butter knife
- Moved to another floor
