The Wisconsin Center is Milwaukee's premier convention facility, situated in the heart of downtown adjacent to the Hyatt Regency Milwaukee and within walking distance of the city's Historic Third Ward, the Milwaukee Art Museum, and dozens of restaurants and bars. The center features state-of-the-art meeting rooms, high-speed Wi-Fi, and full catering services.

GETTING TO MILWAUKEE

By Air

Milwaukee Mitchell International Airport (MKE) is the primary airport, located approximately 8 miles south of downtown. Rideshare to the Wisconsin Center takes 15–20 minutes. Taxi service is also available curbside.

Chicago O'Hare (ORD) and Chicago Midway (MDW) are viable alternatives with more flight options, particularly from the coasts. Budget approximately 90 minutes for ground transportation to Milwaukee.

By Train

Amtrak Hiawatha Service connects Chicago Union Station to Milwaukee (Milwaukee Intermodal Station) in approximately 90 minutes. Seven daily departures from Chicago. The Milwaukee station is a 10-minute rideshare from the Wisconsin Center. Highly recommended for attendees arriving from Chicago.

By Car

Milwaukee is served by I-94 (Chicago to Minneapolis corridor), I-43 (Green Bay), and I-894/US-45 (western suburban loop). The Wisconsin Center garage at 4th and Kilbourn offers parking, as do several nearby surface lots and structures. Overnight rates at conference hotels typically include parking.

By Rideshare

Uber and Lyft operate throughout the Milwaukee metro. The Wisconsin Center address (400 W Wisconsin Ave) resolves correctly in both apps. During conference hours, pickup and dropoff is most efficient on W Kilbourn Ave at the north entrance.

AROUND MILWAUKEE

Milwaukee rewards exploration. Here are staff recommendations within reach of the Wisconsin Center.

Milwaukee Art Museum

The Santiago Calatrava-designed Quadracci Pavilion is one of the most photographed buildings in the Midwest. World-class permanent collection. 15-minute walk along the lakefront.

Historic Third Ward

Milwaukee's arts and dining district, 10 minutes from the Wisconsin Center. Outstanding restaurants, craft cocktail bars, and boutique shopping.

Brady Street

A walkable strip of independent restaurants, coffee shops, and bars in Milwaukee's East Side neighborhood. 15-minute rideshare from the venue.

Miller Valley

Free tours of the historic Miller Brewing campus run daily. A genuinely interesting facility and a distinctly Milwaukee experience. 10-minute rideshare.

Milwaukee Public Market

Year-round indoor market in the Third Ward with local food vendors, a wine bar, and excellent Wisconsin cheese selections. Ideal for lunch on a free afternoon.

Lake Michigan

Milwaukee sits on the western shore of Lake Michigan. The lakefront path runs from the Art Museum through Veterans Park and beyond — worth an early morning run or evening walk.
