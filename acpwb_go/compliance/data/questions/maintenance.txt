# Facilities and equipment maintenance practices.
Q: Preventive maintenance is:
+ Scheduled maintenance done to prevent breakdowns
- Fixing things only after they fail
- Maintenance on the weekends only
- A type of insurance

Q: Before maintenance on equipment with hazardous energy, technicians must:
+ Apply lockout/tagout and verify zero energy
- Turn off the lights
- Post a sticky note
- Work quickly

Q: Every maintenance job should be:
+ Recorded in a work order
- Kept in the technician's memory
- Done without records
- Recorded only if it fails

Q: Replacement parts should be:
+ Approved parts that meet the equipment's specifications
- Whatever fits
- Borrowed from another machine
- Homemade

Q: Contractors doing maintenance must:
+ Follow ACPWB's safety programs, including permits where needed
- Bring their own rules
- Skip lockout because they are experts
- Work without supervision

Q: A technician finds a defect that is outside their training. They should:
+ Stop and escalate to a qualified person
- Try to fix it anyway
- Ignore it
- Leave it for the next shift without a note

Q: Maintenance on fire protection systems must be:
+ Coordinated with Security and Facilities, with impairment procedures if systems are shut off
- Done without telling anyone
- Done during fire drills
- Skipped if inconvenient

Q: Tools left behind inside equipment after maintenance can:
+ Cause damage or injury; account for all tools before restart
- Improve performance
- Be collected next year
- Be ignored

Q: Maintenance records are useful because they:
+ Show history, support warranty claims, and help predict failures
- Fill filing cabinets
- Are required for parties
- Have no use

Q: Working on electrical equipment requires:
+ Qualified, trained personnel and proper PPE
- Any volunteer
- Rubber boots only
- A strong flashlight

Q: Ladders used for maintenance should be:
+ Inspected before use and the right type and height for the job
- Any ladder nearby
- Extended with boxes
- Used as shelves
