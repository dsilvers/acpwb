VULNERABILITY DISCLOSURE POLICY

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in {PROGRAM}, please report it to {DEPT} at {EMAIL}. Please include the release number ({REL}), steps to reproduce, and the percentile you were able to obtain.

We will acknowledge your report within {SMALLN} business days. We do not offer a bug bounty. We offer {SNACK}.

Out of scope: the checksum (known to protect nothing), the percentile ladder (working as designed), and the comments (no fix planned).
