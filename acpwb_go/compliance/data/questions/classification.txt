# Worker classification (employees vs. independent contractors).

Q: Whether a worker is an employee or an independent contractor depends on:
+ The actual working relationship, such as control and economic dependence, not just the contract's label
- What the worker prefers
- Whether they have a business card
- The color of their badge

Q: A "contractor" who works full-time, uses ACPWB equipment, follows a set schedule, and is supervised like staff:
+ May be misclassified and actually be an employee
- Is definitely a contractor
- Must be paid in cash
- Is a consultant

Q: Misclassifying employees as contractors can result in:
+ Back wages, overtime, taxes, penalties, and benefits liability
- A bonus for the department
- Nothing, if the contract is signed
- A warning only

Q: Contingent workers provided by a staffing agency should:
+ Be managed through the agency for matters like discipline and pay
- Receive ACPWB stock awards
- Be added to the internal employee directory as employees
- Be invited to vote in the {COMMITTEE}

Q: An unpaid internship at a for-profit company is generally lawful only when:
+ The intern, not the employer, is the primary beneficiary of the arrangement
- The intern agrees in writing
- The intern is a relative
- The internship is shorter than {DAYS} days

Q: Under the Department of Labor's approach, a key question for classification is whether the worker is:
+ Economically dependent on the company or truly in business for themselves
- Friendly with the manager
- Paid by check
- Over 25

Q: A former employee rehired to do the same job as a "contractor" is:
+ A misclassification red flag
- A standard cost saving
- Required after a layoff
- Allowed if they agree

Q: Independent contractors generally:
+ Control how they do the work, use their own tools, and can work for others
- Have ACPWB email signatures and business cards
- Attend mandatory staff meetings
- Get performance reviews from ACPWB managers

Q: California's "ABC test" presumes a worker is an employee unless:
+ The worker is free from control, does work outside the usual course of the business, and has an independent business
- They sign a contract
- They work from home
- They are paid weekly
