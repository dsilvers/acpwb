- Pajamas with a blazer
- Swimwear on Fridays
- A tuxedo

Q: On "casual Friday," jeans are:
+ Allowed if neat and in good condition, unless you are meeting clients who expect business attire
- Prohibited
- Required
- Allowed only if they are ACPWB branded

+ Religious attire and disability-related needs, absent undue hardship
- Favorite sports teams
- Halloween every day
- Anyone who asks the {COMMITTEE}

Q: Clothing with offensive slogans or images is:
+ Not allowed at work
- Allowed if it is funny
- Allowed on {WEEKDAY}s

+ Take priority over style; follow the posted PPE rules
- Can be ignored for fashion
- Apply only in winter
- Allow flip-flops

+ Consistently, without targeting particular groups
- Only to employees the manager dislikes
- Differently by gender for similar roles
- Only on the first day

+ Between you and your webcam, as long as you do not stand up

- Required for comfort
- Recorded in {SYSTEM}

+ Used sparingly or avoided, out of consideration for coworkers with sensitivities
