ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

{HOLDER} is committed to making its software accessible to people with disabilities, consistent with Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act. {PROGRAM} produces fixed-width text output, which is compatible with screen readers, braille displays, and anyone with a ruler.
