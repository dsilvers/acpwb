+ Biased sampling and not appropriate

- Required
- Random

- Delete them

- Make no difference

+ Improper
- Helpful
- Required
- Normal

Q: A population for sampling should be:

- Partial
- Filtered by management
- Invented

+ Groups, such as by dollar amount, before sampling
- Floors of the building
- Random letters
- Nothing

Q: Sampling documentation should show:
+ How the sample was selected and the results
- Only the results
- Nothing
- Only the auditor's name
