# Sarbanes-Oxley and internal controls over financial reporting.

Q: Section 302 of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act requires the CEO and CFO to:
+ Certify the accuracy of periodic financial reports and the effectiveness of disclosure controls
- Personally audit every expense report
- Sign every paycheck
- Attend every {EVENT}

Q: Section 404 of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act requires management to:
+ Assess and report on the effectiveness of internal control over financial reporting
- Reduce the number of controls
- Hire more accountants every year
- Publish all internal emails

Q: "Segregation of duties" means:
+ No single person controls all steps of a transaction, such as requesting, approving, and recording a payment
- Employees sit in separate rooms
- Duties are divided by alphabetical order

+ Document it and report it so it can be evaluated and remediated

- Hide it until after the audit
- Ask a coworker to sign off instead

Q: A "material weakness" is:
+ A deficiency that creates a reasonable possibility that a material misstatement will not be prevented or detected in time
- A sore back from lifting binders
- A weak password
- Any spelling error in the annual report

+ Falsification of records and may violate federal law
- An acceptable cleanup step
- Allowed if the control was performed eventually
- Allowed with a manager's verbal approval

+ Fines and imprisonment of up to 20 years
- A written warning
- A reduced bonus
- Nothing, if the documents were drafts

Q: Evidence of a control's performance should be:
+ Retained so that it can be reviewed by management and auditors
- Thrown away after the control is performed
- Kept in your personal email
- Stored on a sticky note

+ A segregation of duties conflict
- An efficient workflow

+ A red flag that should be reviewed
