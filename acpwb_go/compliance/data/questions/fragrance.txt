# Fragrance-free and scent-sensitive workplace guidelines.
Q: Strong fragrances can cause problems for coworkers with:
+ Allergies, asthma, or migraines
- Good taste
- Hunger
- Sunburn

Q: ACPWB's scent guidelines ask employees to:
+ Use fragrances lightly or not at all in shared spaces
- Wear strong perfume daily
- Spray air freshener at desks
- Burn candles

Q: Plug-in air fresheners and candles in offices are:
+ Not allowed under fire safety and scent guidelines
- Encouraged
- Required
- Allowed near paper

Q: A coworker asks you to reduce a scent that affects them. You should:
+ Respond respectfully and adjust where you can
- Ignore them
- Spray more
- Report them

Q: Fragrance sensitivity may require:
+ A reasonable accommodation, handled through HR
- No action
- Firing the employee
- A vote

Q: Scented cleaning products used by Facilities should be:
+ Chosen with scent sensitivities in mind
- As strong as possible
- Hidden
- Sprayed in meetings

Q: Strong-smelling foods in shared areas:
+ Are fine in moderation, with consideration for others
- Are prohibited completely
- Must be eaten in meetings
- Must be heated for an hour

Q: Complaints about scents should be handled:
+ Respectfully, without singling people out publicly
- With public shaming
- With signs naming the person
- Never

Q: Essential oil diffusers at desks are:
+ Not allowed in shared spaces
- Required
- Encouraged
- Allowed in elevators

Q: Visitors with scent sensitivities should:
+ Be accommodated with meeting spaces where possible
- Be ignored
- Be told to leave
- Be given perfume samples
