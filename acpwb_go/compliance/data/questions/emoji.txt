# Emoji, reactions, and GIFs in business communication.

Q: Reacting with a thumbs-up emoji to a request in chat usually means:
+ You saw it and agree or will do it, so do not use it if you will not
- You strongly disagree
- You are on vacation
- You want a meeting

Q: A thumbs-up reaction on a contract change in some court cases has been treated as:
+ Possible acceptance of the agreement, so react carefully to business terms
- Meaningless in all cases
- An official signature required by {AGENCY}
- A request for a refund

Q: The "eyes" emoji reaction on a message typically means:
+ "I'm looking at this"
- "I am crying"
- "This is spooky"
- "Please escalate to the {COMMITTEE}"

Q: Using emoji in an email to a regulator or an external auditor is:
+ Usually inappropriate; keep formal communication formal
- Required for friendliness
- Encouraged to build rapport
- Required by {AGENCY}

Q: A skull emoji (💀) from a younger colleague usually means:
+ Something was extremely funny
- A safety incident occurred
- They are threatening you
- They want to discuss retirement plans

Q: Sending a GIF in a channel announcing a coworker's family emergency is:
+ Inappropriate; respond with a simple, sincere message
- A great way to lighten the mood
- Required by policy
- Encouraged on {WEEKDAY}s

Q: The "white check mark" ✅ reaction is commonly used to show:
+ The task is done
- The message is wrong
- You are taking the afternoon off
- You want a refund

Q: Custom emoji uploaded to the ACPWB chat workspace must:
+ Follow the Code of Conduct and not mock coworkers
- Include at least one coworker's face
- Be approved by {VENDOR}
- Be animated

Q: Ending every message with "🙂" can come across as:
+ Passive-aggressive to some readers, depending on context
- Legally binding
- A security risk
- A request for overtime

Q: Emoji in a performance review are:
+ Not appropriate
- Required for "Exceeds Expectations"
- Allowed only as 🎉
- Allowed if the review is positive

Q: A coworker uses an emoji you find offensive. A good first step is to:
+ Let them know privately, or report it if it is harassment
- Reply with a worse emoji
- Leave the channel forever
- Screenshot it for the holiday party

Q: The "party popper" 🎉 is appropriate for:
+ Celebrating wins and milestones
- Layoff announcements
- Incident reports
- Condolence messages
