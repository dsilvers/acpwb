# Workplace chat (Slack, Teams, and the ACPWB Internal Messenger).

Q: [Using @channel|Notifying everyone in the channel] to announce that there is cake in the {PLACE} is:
+ Discouraged; it notifies hundreds of people, some of whom are asleep
- Encouraged, since cake is time-sensitive
- Required by the {COMMITTEE}
- Allowed only for carrot cake

Q: A message that says only "hey" or "got a sec?" with no context:
+ Should include the question, so the recipient can answer when they are available
- Is the preferred greeting at ACPWB
- Must be answered within 30 seconds
- Should be followed by "?" every minute until answered

Q: Replies to a specific message in a busy channel should be:
+ Kept in a thread
- Posted as new messages so everyone sees them
- Sent as a direct message to the whole team
- Printed and posted in the {PLACE}

Q: Customer account numbers and Social Security numbers may be posted in chat:
+ Never in open channels; use approved secure systems for sensitive data
- In any channel with fewer than 50 members
- If followed by the "eyes" emoji
- In the #random channel only

Q: [Your chat status|Your status] reads "In a meeting" but you are on vacation. You should:
+ Set an out-of-office status and, if needed, a note on who is covering
- Leave it, to look busy
- Change it to "Always available"
- Set it to "In a meeting" permanently

Q: You accidentally post a confidential {DOCUMENT} to the wrong channel. You should:
+ Delete it, tell the channel owner and your manager, and report it per the incident process
- Leave it and hope no one notices
- Post more messages so it scrolls out of view
- Rename the channel

Q: Reacting to a manager's announcement of layoffs with the "party popper" emoji is:
+ Likely to be read as insensitive
- A sign of strong engagement
- Required to acknowledge receipt
- Neutral, because emojis have no meaning

Q: Messages sent in workplace chat:
+ May be retained and reviewed under ACPWB records and eDiscovery policies
- Disappear forever after 24 hours
- Are private, even in company channels
- Are never read by anyone, including the recipient

Q: Before messaging a colleague [late at night|on a weekend|in another time zone], consider:
+ Scheduling the message for their working hours
- Calling them instead to be sure they see it
- Marking it urgent to get a faster reply
- Sending it to their personal phone number
