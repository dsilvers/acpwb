# Employment eligibility verification (Form I-9). Answers follow USCIS
# guidance in general terms.
Q: Form I-9 must be completed for:
+ Every new employee hired in the United States, citizens and noncitizens alike
- Only noncitizens
- Only part-time employees
- Only employees who look foreign

Q: The employee must complete Section 1 of Form I-9:
+ No later than the first day of employment
- Within 90 days
- At the end of the first year
- Only if asked

Q: The employer must complete Section 2 of Form I-9:
+ Within three business days of the employee's first day of work
- Within 30 days
- At the employee's first review
- Never

Q: When presenting documents for Form I-9, the employee:
+ May choose which acceptable documents to present
- Must present a passport
- Must present exactly what the employer asks for
- Must present a birth certificate

Q: Asking a lawful permanent resident to show a green card specifically, when they presented other acceptable documents, is:
+ Document abuse and may be unlawful discrimination
- Required
- A best practice
- Fine if done politely

Q: A combination of a List B document and a List C document can be used instead of:
+ A single List A document
- Section 1
- A Social Security number
- A background check

Q: Employers must keep each Form I-9 for:
+ Three years after the hire date or one year after employment ends, whichever is later
- 30 days
- Forever
- Until the employee's first promotion

Q: Documents presented for Form I-9 must be:
+ Unexpired and appear genuine and to relate to the employee
- Photocopies only
- Laminated
- Expired, to prove history

Q: E-Verify participants that meet the requirements may examine I-9 documents:
+ Remotely using the DHS-authorized alternative procedure
- Over text message
- Never; only in person forever
- Through the employee's friends

Q: Rehiring a former employee within three years of the original Form I-9 allows the employer to:
+ Use Supplement B to update or reverify instead of completing a new form
- Skip all verification forever
- Use the employee's old badge as proof
- Ask a coworker to vouch

Q: Completing Form I-9 only for applicants who "look foreign" is:
+ Unlawful discrimination
- Efficient
- Required by law
- Allowed in some states
