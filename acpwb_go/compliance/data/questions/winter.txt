# Cold stress and winter safety. Correct answers follow CDC/NIOSH and NWS
# guidance; keep them accurate.

Q: Signs of frostbite include:
+ Numbness and skin that looks white, grayish-yellow, or waxy
- Bright red cheeks from laughing
- Sweating
- A strong urge to sing

Q: Frostbitten skin should NOT be:
+ Rubbed or massaged, or warmed with a heating pad or fire
- Protected from further cold
- Warmed gently with warm (not hot) water if medical care is delayed
- Seen by a medical professional

Q: Shivering, confusion, slurred speech, and drowsiness in the cold are signs of:
+ Hypothermia, which is a medical emergency
- Normal Monday fatigue
- Too much coffee
- A need for more training

Q: For someone with suspected hypothermia, you should:
+ Call 911, move them somewhere warm, remove wet clothing, and warm the center of the body first
- Give them alcohol to warm up
- Rub their arms and legs vigorously
- Have them run laps

Q: The wind chill describes:
+ How cold it feels on exposed skin when wind is combined with temperature
- The temperature inside the {PLACE}
- A drink served at {EVENT}
- The speed of the elevator

Q: When walking on icy sidewalks, you should:
+ Take short, shuffling steps and walk flat-footed, like a penguin
- Run to get inside faster
- Carry heavy boxes in both arms
- Text while walking to stay calm

Q: A car stuck in snow with the engine running should:
+ Have the exhaust pipe cleared of snow to prevent carbon monoxide buildup
- Be kept sealed with the windows up and the exhaust blocked
- Be abandoned with the engine running
- Be warmed with a charcoal grill inside

Q: Layered clothing in the cold helps because:
+ Layers trap warm air and can be adjusted as you warm up or cool down
- It makes you look busy
- It is required by policy
- One thick cotton layer is always best

Q: Shoveling heavy snow can be risky because:
+ It is strenuous and can trigger heart problems, so pace yourself and take breaks
- Snow is slippery only on Tuesdays
- Shovels are sharp
- It makes the {COMMITTEE} angry

Q: Using a gas generator or grill inside a garage during a winter power outage is:
+ Dangerous because of carbon monoxide poisoning, even with the door open
- Safe with the door open
- Safe for short periods
- Recommended for warmth

Q: Wet clothing in the cold should be:
+ Changed as soon as possible, because wet clothes pull heat from the body
- Kept on to dry naturally
- Covered with a dry coat only
- Ignored
