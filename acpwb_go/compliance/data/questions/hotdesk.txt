# Desk sharing / hoteling / neighborhoods.

Q: [Under the Desk Sharing Standard|In a shared-desk neighborhood], desks must be:
+ Booked in advance through the reservation tool and cleared at the end of each day
- Claimed by leaving a jacket on the chair
- Kept by whoever arrives first each week
- Reserved for the full quarter at once

Q: [At the end of the day|Before leaving], [you|associates using a shared desk] must:
+ Remove all personal items and papers, wipe down the desk, and store belongings in a locker
- Leave a sticky note saying "back tomorrow"
- Leave the monitor cable unplugged for the next person to find
- Turn the chair to face the wall

Q: [You booked a desk|Your desk reservation] for {WEEKDAY} but your plans change. You should:
+ Release the booking as soon as you know, so someone else can use it
- Keep it in case you change your mind
- Give it to a friend by letting them sign in as you
- Ignore it; unused bookings expire eventually

Q: [Someone|A colleague] is sitting at the desk you reserved. The best approach is to:
+ Politely show them your reservation and, if needed, ask the neighborhood host or Facilities for help
- Sit on the edge of the desk until they leave
- Remove their belongings while they are away
- Report them to the Integrity Line immediately

Q: [Monitors, docks, and keyboards|Shared desk equipment] at a hot desk should be:
+ Left connected and in working order, with faults reported to IT
- Taken home for safekeeping
- Unplugged and stacked under the desk
- Rearranged to your personal preference and left that way

Q: [Food|Lunch] at a shared desk is:
+ Allowed if you clean up completely, though the {PLACE} is preferred for meals
- Prohibited by federal law
- Only allowed if you share it
- Only allowed on {WEEKDAY}s

Q: [Neighborhood zones|Team neighborhoods] on the {FLOOR} floor are intended to:
+ Seat teams near each other on their in-office days
- Assign permanent desks by seniority
- Separate departments that disagree
- Provide parking for visitors

Q: [Booking a desk|Reserving a desk] you do not plan to use, so that your team "looks busy," is:
+ Not permitted; it blocks desks others need and skews space planning
- Encouraged during audits
- Allowed if you book fewer than {N} desks
- Required for managers

Q: [A colleague|{NAME}] leaves a confidential {DOCUMENT} at a shared desk overnight. The next person to use the desk should:
+ Put it in a confidential shred bin or hand it to Security, without reading it
- Read it to identify the owner
- Leave it where it is
- Take a photo for the team chat

Q: Lockers for associates who share desks are:
+ Assigned or booked through Facilities, and must be emptied according to the posted schedule
- First come, first served, permanently
- For storing food over the weekend
- Available only to people managers
