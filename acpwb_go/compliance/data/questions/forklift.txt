# Powered industrial trucks. Answers follow OSHA 1910.178.

Q: Forklifts must be examined for defects:
+ Before being placed in service each day, or each shift if used around the clock
- Once a year

Q: When a forklift is parked and left unattended, the forks should be:
+ Fully lowered, controls neutralized, power off, and brakes set
- Raised to keep them clean
- Left loaded
- Pointed at the door

Q: Riding as a passenger on a forklift is:
+ Not allowed unless the truck is equipped with a seat for a passenger
- Fine for short distances
- Allowed on the forks
- Allowed for supervisors

Q: When driving a loaded forklift up or down a ramp, the load should be:
+ Upgrade, facing up the ramp
- Downgrade, facing down the ramp
- Raised as high as possible
- Swung side to side

- Drive forward faster
- Lean out of the cab
- Close their eyes

Q: At blind corners and intersections, forklift operators should:
+ Slow down and sound the horn
- Speed up to clear the area
- Flash the headlights only
- Assume pedestrians will move

+ Under 18 years old
- Under 21

Q: A forklift's rated capacity is listed:
+ On the truck's data plate, along with the load center
