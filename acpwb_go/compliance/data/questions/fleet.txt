# Company vehicles and fleet use.
Q: Before driving a company vehicle, you must:
+ Hold a valid license, be authorized, and do a quick walk-around inspection
- Only find the keys
- Adjust the radio
- Ask a friend to drive instead

Q: Using a handheld phone while driving a company vehicle is:
+ Prohibited
- Allowed at red lights
- Allowed for work calls
- Allowed below 25 mph

Q: Passengers in a company vehicle must be:
+ Authorized under the fleet policy and wearing seat belts
- Anyone who needs a ride
- Seated in the cargo area if it is full
- Family members on weekends

Q: An accident in a company vehicle must be:
+ Reported to police where required and to the fleet manager immediately
- Repaired privately to avoid paperwork
- Reported at the end of the month
- Kept quiet if no one was hurt

Q: Traffic tickets received while driving a company vehicle are:
+ The driver's responsibility, and must be reported under the fleet policy
- Paid by the company automatically
- Ignored because the car is company-owned
- Transferred to the last driver

Q: A warning light on the dashboard appears during a trip. You should:
+ Stop when safe and contact the fleet manager or roadside assistance
- Keep driving until it goes away
- Cover it with tape
- Turn up the radio

Q: Personal use of a company vehicle:
+ Is allowed only as the policy permits, and may be taxable
- Is unlimited
- Is never tracked
- Includes lending it to friends

Q: Driving a company vehicle after drinking alcohol is:
+ Prohibited
- Fine after one drink
- Allowed after company parties
- Allowed on private roads

Q: Telematics in company vehicles may record:
+ Location, speed, and harsh braking, as disclosed in the fleet policy
- Your private thoughts
- Nothing at all
- Only the radio station

Q: Fuel cards issued with company vehicles may be used for:
+ Fuel for that company vehicle only
- Personal vehicles
- Snacks and drinks
- Gift cards

Q: Cargo in a company van must be:
+ Secured so it cannot shift during braking
- Loose for easy access
- Stacked above the seat backs
- Held by a passenger
