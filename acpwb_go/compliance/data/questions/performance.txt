# Performance reviews, feedback, and performance improvement plans.

Q: Feedback is most useful when it is:
+ Specific, timely, and focused on behavior and impact
- Saved for the annual review, all at once
- Vague, to avoid conflict
- Delivered in the #general channel

Q: A self-evaluation should:
+ Give an honest account of results, with examples
- Rate yourself "Exceeds Expectations" in every category, to start negotiations high
- Be copied from last year's
- Be written by {VENDOR}

Q: A performance improvement plan (PIP) should include:
+ Clear expectations, measurable goals, support, and a timeline
- No goals, to keep things flexible
- A surprise ending
- A requirement to attend {EVENT}

Q: Ratings in a calibration session should be based on:
+ Documented results and behaviors
- Who speaks most loudly for their team
- Whose manager brought the bagels
- Alphabetical order

Q: Giving a lower rating to an employee because they took protected leave is:
+ Unlawful retaliation
- A way to reward people who were present
- Allowed if the leave was longer than {DAYS} days
- Required for fairness

Q: The phrase "meets expectations" in an ACPWB review means:
+ The employee is doing the job well
- The employee is about to be placed on a PIP
- The reviewer did not read the self-evaluation
- The expectations were very low

Q: An employee disagrees with their review. They should:
+ Discuss it with their manager and, if needed, use the HR review process
- Post the review on social media
- Rewrite the review themselves in {SYSTEM}
- Give their manager a review in return

Q: Recency bias in reviews means:
+ Giving too much weight to recent events instead of the whole period
- Reviewing only new employees
- Writing reviews quickly
- Rating everyone the same

Q: The "halo effect" in reviews means:
+ One positive trait inflates the rating of everything else
- Rating angels
- Giving everyone high ratings
- Lighting in the review room

Q: Feedback about personality, such as "abrasive," is more useful when:
+ Replaced with specific behaviors and their impact
- Repeated louder
- Shared with the team
- Written in all caps
