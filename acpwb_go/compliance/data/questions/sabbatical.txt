# Sabbatical program (ACPWB policy).

+ Years of service and the program's criteria
- Popularity
- Random lottery
- Job title only

Q: Sabbatical requests should be:
+ Submitted well in advance so coverage can be planned
- Announced the day before
- Kept secret
- Taken without approval

Q: During a sabbatical, employees should:
+ Not perform work unless the plan says otherwise
- Check email daily
- Attend every meeting
- Work for a competitor

Q: Benefits during a paid sabbatical:
+ Generally continue as described in the policy
- End immediately
- Double
- Are paid in cash

Q: Taking a sabbatical to work for a competitor is:
+ Not allowed
- Encouraged
- Fine if unpaid
- Required

Q: A coverage plan for a sabbatical should include:
+ Who handles responsibilities and how to escalate
- Nothing
- The employee's password
- A list of complaints

Q: Returning from sabbatical, employees can expect:
+ To return to their role as described in the policy
- A new job automatically
- No job
- A promotion

Q: Sabbatical time is different from:
+ Medical or family leave, which have their own rules
- Vacation in every way
- Nothing
- A lunch break

Q: Managers should approve sabbaticals:
+ Fairly and consistently under the program criteria
- Only for friends
- Never
- Randomly

Q: Projects during a sabbatical, such as volunteering, should:
+ Avoid conflicts of interest
- Use company confidential data
- Involve competitors
- Be secret
