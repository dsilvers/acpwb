# Potlucks, shared food, and team lunches.

Q: Dishes brought to a potluck should be:
+ Labeled with their name and major allergens
- Mystery dishes for fun
- Unlabeled to avoid judgment
- Brought in unmarked containers

Q: Hot potluck dishes should be kept:
+ At or above 140°F, and cold dishes at or below 40°F
- At room temperature all day
- In a desk drawer
- In the sun

Q: Leftover potluck food that sat out more than two hours should be:
+ Thrown away
- Taken home for dinner
- Refrigerated and served tomorrow
- Shared with the next meeting

Q: A coworker with a severe peanut allergy attends a potluck. Others should:
+ Label dishes clearly and avoid cross-contamination with serving utensils
- Ignore it, since it is their responsibility alone
- Bring only peanut dishes
- Hide the labels

Q: Team lunches paid with a corporate card require:
+ A business purpose, a list of attendees, and an itemized receipt
- Nothing
- A photo of the food
- Approval from {VENDOR}

Q: Alcohol at a team lunch on a workday is:
+ Not allowed unless approved under the events policy
- Encouraged
- Required on Fridays
- Allowed for managers only

Q: A potluck sign-up should:
+ Be voluntary, and accommodate dietary needs and religious practices
- Require everyone to cook
- Require pork dishes
- Assign dishes by seniority

Q: Shared serving spoons should be:
+ Used for one dish only, to prevent cross-contact
- Shared across dishes
- Licked between dishes
- Replaced with fingers
