# Daily stand-up meetings.
Q: A daily stand-up should be:
+ Short and focused on progress, plans, and blockers
- An hour-long status report
- Optional for everyone
- A problem-solving marathon

Q: Blockers raised in stand-up should be:
+ Followed up after the meeting by the right people
- Ignored
- Solved in the stand-up at length
- Hidden

Q: Stand-ups work best when:
+ Everyone is on time and prepared
- People arrive late
- Nobody speaks
- Managers interrogate

Q: Detailed discussions during stand-up should be:
+ Taken offline after the meeting
- Continued until done
- Required
- Forbidden forever

Q: Remote team members in stand-ups should:
+ Be able to participate fully
- Be ignored
- Listen only
- Turn off audio

Q: Stand-ups are not for:
+ Blaming people
- Sharing progress
- Raising blockers
- Coordinating

Q: Updates in stand-up should be:
+ Honest about progress and problems
- Always positive
- Vague
- Invented

Q: Async stand-ups can work for:
+ Teams across time zones
- Nobody
- Only managers
- Only weekends

Q: Stand-up time should be:
+ Consistent and respected
- Random
- Late at night
- Extended daily

Q: If stand-ups feel useless, the team should:
+ Discuss it in a retrospective and adjust
- Skip silently
- Complain
- Quit
