# Pets in the office (Bring Your Dog to Work Day, ACPWB Policy {POLICY}).

Q: Before bringing a dog to the office on Bring Your Dog to Work Day, you must:
+ Register the dog and confirm it is vaccinated, house-trained, and comfortable around people
- Teach it the Code of Conduct
- Request a badge photo for the dog
- Book a meeting room for its naps

Q: A service animal, as defined by the ADA, is:
+ A dog individually trained to do work or perform tasks for a person with a disability, and is not a pet
- Any animal that makes its owner feel better
- An animal registered online for a fee
- The ACPWB mascot

Q: Asking an employee to "demonstrate" what their service dog does is:
+ Not allowed
- Required at the front desk
- Allowed during {EVENT}
- Encouraged as team building

Q: [A colleague|{NAME}] is allergic to dogs. On Bring Your Dog to Work Day:
+ Pet-free zones and seating must be respected, and dogs kept away from them
- The colleague should work from the parking lot
- Dogs take priority over people
- The colleague must bring a cat to balance things out

Q: Dogs in the office should be:
+ Leashed or in an assigned area, and never left unattended
- Allowed to roam freely to network
- Allowed in the {PLACE} kitchen to clean up crumbs
- Allowed to attend {COMMITTEE} meetings as voting members

Q: A dog has an accident in the {PLACE}. The owner should:
+ Clean it up right away and report it to Facilities if carpet cleaning is needed
- Blame another dog
- Leave a note for the overnight crew
- Cover it with a recycling bin

Q: Under the ADA, staff may ask only two questions about a service animal:
+ Is the dog a service animal required because of a disability, and what work or task has it been trained to perform?
- What is your disability and can I see the paperwork?
- How much did the dog cost and where was it trained?
- Can it do a trick?

Q: Emotional support animals in the workplace:
+ May be considered as a reasonable accommodation under the ADA's employment rules
- Are always required
- Are never allowed
- Are the same as service animals for all purposes

Q: A dog that behaves aggressively at the office should be:
+ Removed from the office
- Given a treat
- Put in a meeting room
- Ignored

Q: Pet food left in the kitchen should be:
+ Stored sealed and away from human food
- Mixed with snacks
- Left open
- Shared with coworkers

Q: Cats, birds, and reptiles on Bring Your Dog to Work Day are:
+ Not included unless the policy says so
- Encouraged
- Required
- Allowed in the server room
