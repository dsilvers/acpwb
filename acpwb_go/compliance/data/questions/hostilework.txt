# Hostile work environment harassment. Answers follow EEOC guidance.
Q: A hostile work environment exists when harassment based on a protected characteristic is:
+ Severe or pervasive enough that a reasonable person would find the workplace hostile or abusive
- Any rude comment once
- Any disagreement with a manager
- Any unpopular schedule change

Q: Conduct that can contribute to a hostile work environment includes:
+ Slurs, offensive jokes, threats, and offensive images tied to a protected characteristic
- Assigning normal work
- Giving honest performance feedback
- Scheduling meetings

Q: A single incident can create a hostile environment when it is:
+ Extremely serious, such as a physical assault or a threat
- Mildly annoying
- A missed lunch invitation
- A typo in an email

Q: Courts evaluate a hostile environment claim by looking at:
+ The frequency and severity of the conduct, whether it was threatening or humiliating, and whether it interfered with work
- Only the harasser's intent
- Only whether others laughed
- Only the victim's job title

Q: An employer can be liable for coworker harassment when it:
+ Knew or should have known about it and failed to take prompt, appropriate action
- Never heard about it
- Had a written policy
- Fired the victim

Q: Harassment by a customer that management knows about:
+ Must be addressed by the employer
- Is the employee's problem
- Is allowed because customers are always right
- Is outside every policy

Q: Offensive jokes in a team chat channel:
+ Can contribute to a hostile work environment
- Are private and harmless
- Are protected free speech at work
- Are required for team bonding

Q: Witnesses to ongoing harassment should:
+ Report it, even if they are not the target
- Wait for the target to complain
- Join in to fit in
- Avoid the target

Q: An employee who feels they work in a hostile environment should:
+ Report it through HR, a manager, or the Ethics Hotline
- Quit without telling anyone
- Retaliate
- Post about it publicly first

Q: Investigating a hostile environment complaint should be:
+ Prompt, impartial, and documented
- Delayed until the busy season ends
- Done by the accused person
- Skipped if the accused is a top performer

Q: Corrective action after a substantiated complaint should be:
+ Designed to stop the harassment and prevent it from happening again
- Moving the complainant to a worse job
- A verbal warning to everyone
- Nothing if the harasser apologizes
