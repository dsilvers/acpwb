# Lost and found.
Q: Items found in the office should be:
+ Turned in to Security or reception
- Kept
- Sold
- Thrown away

Q: A found laptop or phone should be:
+ Turned in without looking through it
- Unlocked to find the owner
- Kept
- Wiped

Q: A found badge should be:
+ Turned in to Security right away so it can be checked
- Used
- Kept
- Thrown away

Q: USB drives found in the parking lot should be:
+ Turned in to IT Security, not plugged in
- Plugged in to find the owner
- Kept
- Thrown away

Q: Found documents with confidential information should be:
+ Turned in to Security or the document's owner
- Read
- Shared
- Recycled

Q: Unclaimed lost-and-found items are:
+ Handled under the lost-and-found policy after a holding period
- Kept by finders
- Sold by staff
- Kept forever

Q: Lost company equipment must be:
+ Reported promptly
- Replaced quietly
- Ignored
- Hidden

Q: Found cash should be:
+ Turned in and logged
- Kept
- Spent
- Split

Q: Found keys should be:
+ Turned in to Security or Facilities
- Tested in doors
- Kept
- Copied

Q: Lost-and-found logs help:
+ Return items and show accountability
- Nothing
- Hide items
- Waste time
